# Hidden Fuji viewpoints, EAST / SOUTH-EAST side: Izu Peninsula (West Izu, Numazu), Hakone/Gotemba, Kanagawa coast

Status: research notes as of 20 Nov 2026. IMPORTANT method caveat: in this session only web *search* worked; direct page fetches of Japanese sites (tabi-mag.jp, minnacamera.com etc.) were blocked by the network allowlist, so facts below come from search-result extracts of the cited pages, not from full-page reads. GPS coordinates are my own approximations (NOT taken from a source) and must be checked on a map before publishing. Items marked [UNVERIFIED] or [INFERENCE] are not source-backed.

Selected spots (9): 1 Kumomi coast + Eboshi-yama, 2 Ida / Kirameki-no-oka (+ Heda Mihama-misaki / Deai-misaki), 3 Osezaki, 4 Daruma-yama & Nishi-Izu Skyline, 5 Kanuki-yama (Numazu), 6 Otome-toge + Mt Kintoki, 7 Gotemba Heiwa Koen, 8 Tateishi Park (Akiya), 9 Morito / Manase coast (Hayama).
Dropped / demoted: Dogashima (no source found showing a strong Fuji composition), Koganezaki (kept only as a short backup: Fuji is a distant secondary element, no free Fuji photo found), Jukkoku-toge and Mikuni-toge/Ashinoko Skyline (well-known drive-up panoramas; the only free Jukkoku photo was weak and was deleted), Inamuragasaki and Chigasaki (popular, not hidden; only Diamond-Fuji dates kept).

## Which of these are actually uncrowded, and what does the shot look like (foreground, focal length, direction of light)?

### Takeaway
The West-Izu coast spots (Kumomi, Ida, Osezaki, Daruma-yama) give the rarest composition: Fuji rising straight out of Suruga Bay with rocks, a fishing hamlet or a ridge road as foreground, and they are far from the Tokyo day-trip circuit. On the Kanagawa side Tateishi and Morito/Manase are local favourites rather than secrets: quiet on winter weekdays, busy with tripods on Diamond-Fuji evenings.

### Cited Findings

**1. Kumomi coast (雲見海岸, Kumomi-kaigan) and Ushitsuki-iwa (牛着岩), Matsuzaki town; plus Kumomi Sengen shrine on Eboshi-yama (雲見浅間神社 / 烏帽子山)**
- GPS (approx., own estimate): beach 34.718 N, 138.745 E; Eboshi-yama summit deck approx. 34.721 N, 138.741 E.
- Signature shot: the two offshore rocks "Ushitsuki-iwa" (large + small) with Fuji directly behind them across the bay — [co-trip](https://co-trip.jp/spot/788587); the fishery co-op shop page also describes Ushitsuki-iwa as visible right in front — [Shizuoka Pref.](https://www.pref.shizuoka.jp/sangyoshigoto/suisan/suisangyo/uocching/1003392/1043930/1028012.html)
- The downloaded Commons photo confirms the composition: sand beach foreground, the two rocks joined by a shimenawa rope, snow-capped Fuji centred above them; shot from the beach with a short telephoto (portrait format) — [Commons file](https://commons.wikimedia.org/wiki/File:Mount_Fuji_from_Kumomi.jpg)
- Second viewpoint: Eboshi-yama (Kumomi Sengen shrine). Stone steps: 130 steps to the haiden, then 320 steeper steps to the naka-no-miya, then an unpaved narrow path to the honden / summit deck; Fuji is seen well across the bay from the top — [YAMAP report](https://yamap.com/activities/21678453/article), [BE-PAL](https://www.bepal.net/archives/276849)
- A foot bath with Fuji view exists at the coast (reviewer comment) — [4travel](https://4travel.jp/dm_shisetsu/10032137)
- Crowding: a simple fishing/onsen hamlet, busy only in the summer bathing season — [iko-yo](https://iko-yo.net/facilities/27874), [co-trip](https://co-trip.jp/spot/788587)
- Photo: `east_kumomi_1.jpg` — https://commons.wikimedia.org/wiki/File:Mount_Fuji_from_Kumomi.jpg — author Alpsdake — CC BY-SA 4.0 (checked visually: excellent, shows Ushitsuki-iwa + Fuji from the beach).

**2. Ida (井田) – Kirameki-no-oka (煌めきの丘), Numazu (former Heda village); with Heda Mihama-misaki (御浜岬) and Deai-misaki (出逢い岬) as nearby alternates**
- GPS (approx., own estimate): Kirameki-no-oka 34.998 N, 138.776 E; Deai-misaki approx. 34.975 N, 138.770 E; Mihama-misaki approx. 34.973 N, 138.767 E.
- Kirameki-no-oka is a roadside lookout on Prefectural Road 17; looking down on Ida beach with Fuji over the sea, described as one of the best views in Izu; steps below the deck lead to the Matsue kofun group and Myojin-ike pond — [tabi-mag](https://tabi-mag.jp/sz464/), [yukoyuko](https://www.yukoyuko.net/onsen/spot/s0001753)
- The Commons photos confirm the composition: high viewpoint, Ida hamlet and a field in which the characters 井田 are written in yellow rapeseed flowers, steep headland right, Suruga Bay and Fuji behind; second frame shows Myojin-ike pond in the foreground — [Commons file 1](https://commons.wikimedia.org/wiki/File:Mount_Fuji_from_Kiramikinooka.jpg), [Commons file 2](https://commons.wikimedia.org/wiki/File:Nyojin_Pond_and_Mount_Fuji.jpg)
- Deai-misaki: roadside deck on Road 17 overlooking Fuji, Mihama-misaki sand spit and Heda harbour; has a ring-shaped monument that frames Fuji — [rurubu](https://rurubu.jp/andmore/spot/80022020), [nap-camp](https://www.nap-camp.com/mag/38418)
- Mihama-misaki: the outer (open sea) shore of the spit is a "prime spot" for Fuji across the sea; Moroguchi shrine stands near the tip — [rurubu](https://rurubu.jp/andmore/spot/80022023)
- Photos: `east_ida_1.jpg` — https://commons.wikimedia.org/wiki/File:Mount_Fuji_from_Kiramikinooka.jpg — Alpsdake — CC BY-SA 4.0 (good; 井田 flower letters visible). `east_ida_2.jpg` — https://commons.wikimedia.org/wiki/File:Nyojin_Pond_and_Mount_Fuji.jpg — Alpsdake — CC BY-SA 4.0 (good; Myojin pond + Fuji).

**3. Osezaki (大瀬崎, also Ose-zaki / Cape Ose), Numazu**
- GPS (approx., own estimate): cape tip / lighthouse 35.030 N, 138.787 E.
- A sand spit jutting into Suruga Bay with a nationally protected juniper (byakushin) grove and Ose shrine; known as a Fuji photo spot; a footpath circles the cape; the freshwater Kami-ike pond at the tip asks a 100 yen (adult) / 50 yen (child) environmental contribution — [rurubu](https://rurubu.jp/andmore/spot/80021999), [tabi-mag](https://tabi-mag.jp/sz473/)
- Commons photos confirm two compositions from the cape: (a) the small white Osezaki lighthouse on the pebble shore with Fuji and the Ashitaka range across the bay (wide angle; a fence partly intrudes), (b) telephoto of Fuji with breaking surf in the foreground in pink early/late light — [file a](https://commons.wikimedia.org/wiki/File:Osezaki_Lighthouse_and_Mount_Fuji.jpg), [file b](https://commons.wikimedia.org/wiki/File:Mt.Fuji-Osezaki.jpg)
- Photos: `east_osezaki_1.jpg` — https://commons.wikimedia.org/wiki/File:Osezaki_Lighthouse_and_Mount_Fuji.jpg — Alpsdake — CC BY-SA 4.0 (OK; lighthouse + Fuji, fence in frame). `east_osezaki_2.jpg` — https://commons.wikimedia.org/wiki/File:Mt.Fuji-Osezaki.jpg — MaedaAkihiko — CC BY-SA 4.0 (very good; red-lit Fuji + surf).

**4. Daruma-yama (達磨山, 982 m) and Nishi-Izu Skyline (西伊豆スカイライン); Darumayama Kogen rest house (だるま山高原レストハウス)**
- GPS (approx., own estimate): rest house 34.972 N, 138.860 E; Heda-toge pass 34.970 N, 138.835 E; Daruma-yama summit 34.955 N, 138.839 E.
- Nishi-Izu Skyline is a ~10.8 km ridge road, toll-free, with lay-bys and lookouts giving Fuji + Suruga Bay — [nap-camp](https://www.nap-camp.com/mag/107165)
- The best Fuji view is said to be NOT from the summit but from beside the Darumayama rest house, with Suruga Bay under Fuji — the view favoured by painter Yokoyama Taikan; from the summit the bay is partly hidden by foreground hills — [tabi-mag](https://tabi-mag.jp/yokoyamataikan/)
- Commons photos confirm both: (a) from the rest-house terrace: Numazu coast, Suruga Bay and Fuji; (b) from Daruma-yama: bamboo-grass ridge with the winding Skyline road and a footpath leading the eye to Fuji on the left (wide angle, frost/snow on the path) — [file a](https://commons.wikimedia.org/wiki/File:Mount_Fuji_from_Darumayama_Highland.jpg), [file b](https://commons.wikimedia.org/wiki/File:Nishiizu_Skyline_and_Mount_Fuji_from_Mount_Daruma.jpg)
- Crowding: alternative car park at Asebigahara near Heda-toge; one review reports the car park full on a Sunday — [4travel](https://4travel.jp/dm_shisetsu/11309195)
- Photos: `east_darumayama_1.jpg` — https://commons.wikimedia.org/wiki/File:Mount_Fuji_from_Darumayama_Highland.jpg — Alpsdake — CC BY-SA 4.0. `east_darumayama_2.jpg` — https://commons.wikimedia.org/wiki/File:Nishiizu_Skyline_and_Mount_Fuji_from_Mount_Daruma.jpg — Alpsdake — CC BY-SA 4.0 (very good; ridge road + Fuji).

**5. Kanuki-yama (香貫山, 193 m), northern end of the "Numazu Alps", Numazu city**
- GPS (approx., own estimate): summit-area observation deck 35.082 N, 138.877 E; Koryodai (香陵台) car park approx. 35.085 N, 138.875 E.
- The deck near the summit overlooks the whole city with Fuji, the Southern Alps and Hakone — [rurubu](https://rurubu.jp/andmore/spot/80022021/)
- Commons photos confirm: (a) dusk/night view — Numazu city lights and the Kano river below, Mt Ashitaka in the middle with Fuji's snow cap peeking above it; (b) long telephoto of the pink-lit Fuji summit and Hoei crater above the dark Ashitaka ridge — [file a](https://commons.wikimedia.org/wiki/File:Mount_Ashitaka_and_Mount_Fuji_from_Mount_Kanuki.jpg), [file b](https://commons.wikimedia.org/wiki/File:Mount_Fuji_from_Mount_Kanuki.jpg)
- Listed as a (free) night-view spot — [walkerplus](https://www.walkerplus.com/spot/ar0622s313225/price.html) (data dated Nov 2020)
- Photos: `east_kanukiyama_1.jpg` — https://commons.wikimedia.org/wiki/File:Mount_Fuji_from_Mount_Kanuki.jpg — Alpsdake — CC BY-SA 4.0. `east_kanukiyama_2.jpg` — https://commons.wikimedia.org/wiki/File:Mount_Ashitaka_and_Mount_Fuji_from_Mount_Kanuki.jpg — Alpsdake — CC BY-SA 4.0.

**6. Otome-toge (乙女峠) and Mt Kintoki (金時山, 1,212 m), Gotemba / Hakone border**
- GPS (approx., own estimate): roadside lookout at FUJIMI CAFE GOTEMBA (Route 138, Gotemba mouth of Otome tunnel) 35.283 N, 138.985 E; Kintoki summit 35.290 N, 139.004 E.
- Roadside: at the Gotemba-side exit of Otome tunnel (elev. ~800 m) stands FUJIMI CAFE GOTEMBA (formerly Otome-toge Fujimi Chaya; bus stop "Otome-toge"); a path beside it climbs a few minutes to a viewing deck with the "Otome bell" — [tabi-mag](https://tabi-mag.jp/otometouge/), [tabi-mag](https://tabi-mag.jp/sz363/)
- Commons photos confirm: from the Otome deck, a full-height snow-covered Fuji with the Gotemba townscape spread at its foot (normal to short tele); from Kintoki summit, Fuji left of centre over Gotemba basin with low cloud/mist layers — [Otome file](https://commons.wikimedia.org/wiki/File:Mount_Fuji_from_Otome_Pass.jpg), [Kintoki file](https://commons.wikimedia.org/wiki/File:Mount_Fuji_from_Mount_Kintoki_2022-11-22.jpg)
- A second Otome frame (17 Feb 2020) shows Fuji with a cloud band on its left flank above the Gotemba townscape, framed by conifers.
- Photos: `east_otome-toge_2.jpg` — https://commons.wikimedia.org/wiki/File:Otome_toge_kara_no_Fuji_san_to_Gotemba_no_Machinami_20200217.jpg — Makoto_Lab — CC BY 4.0 (good). `east_otome-toge_1.jpg` — https://commons.wikimedia.org/wiki/File:Mount_Fuji_from_Otome_Pass.jpg — Alpsdake — CC BY-SA 4.0 (very good). `east_kintoki_1.jpg` — https://commons.wikimedia.org/wiki/File:Mount_Fuji_from_Mount_Kintoki_2022-11-22.jpg — Alpsdake — CC BY-SA 4.0 (good).

**7. Gotemba Heiwa Koen (御殿場 平和公園, Peace Park) — white stupa (仏舎利塔) + Fuji**
- GPS (approx., own estimate): 35.292 N, 138.955 E. Address: Higashi-Tanaka 3422, Gotemba — [at-s](https://www.at-s.com/spot/article/137371)
- One of the city-designated "Fuji view heritage" (富士山眺望遺産) sites; the white stupa combined with Fuji is the motif; cherry blossoms in spring and autumn colour — [at-s](https://www.at-s.com/spot/article/137371), [gltjp](https://www.gltjp.com/ja/article/item/20797/)
- The Commons photo confirms the composition: tall white stupa on the right, smaller stupa and pruned pines in the foreground, bare cherry trees, Gotemba town below and a large snow-covered Fuji on the left (normal lens, ~40-50 mm equivalent; shot from the upper terrace looking west-northwest) — [Commons file](https://commons.wikimedia.org/wiki/File:Stupa_and_Fuji.jpg)
- Photo: `east_gotemba-heiwa-koen_1.jpg` — https://commons.wikimedia.org/wiki/File:Stupa_and_Fuji.jpg — author: Commons user "Jungle" (no machine-readable author; assumed from copyright claim) — CC BY 2.5 (good; older compact-camera image, 2048 px original, but shows exactly the stupa + Fuji motif).

**8. Tateishi Park (立石公園), Akiya, Yokosuka (Miura Peninsula west coast)**
- GPS (approx., own estimate): 35.236 N, 139.603 E. Address given as Akiya 3-5 (another listing: Akiya 3-1584), Yokosuka — [tabi-mag](https://tabi-mag.jp/kn0549/), [walkerplus](https://summer.walkerplus.com/odekake/detail/ar0314s64595/data.html)
- Composition: the ~12 m sea stack "Tateishi", Sagami Bay and distant Fuji in one frame; at dusk the rock becomes a silhouette against the afterglow; winter gives the clearest Fuji — [minnacamera](https://www.minnacamera.com/spots/1015)
- Photo: `east_tateishi_1.jpg` — https://commons.wikimedia.org/wiki/File:葉山・立石公園から見た富士山_Pcs34560_IMG_2309.jpg — author pcs34560 — Public domain. WEAK: hazy pink dusk, Fuji only a faint silhouette, the Tateishi rock itself is not in frame.
- Photo (location reference only, NO Fuji visible): `east_tateishi_3.jpg` — https://commons.wikimedia.org/wiki/File:Akiya_Tateisi_Koen.JPG — author Mizunoumi — CC BY-SA 3.0. Taken from the park car park on an overcast, stormy day: shows the Tateishi sea stack, the pine on the Bonten-no-hana rocks and the surf, i.e. the foreground of the classic shot; Fuji would sit on the horizon behind/right of the rock on a clear day [INFERENCE]. No free Commons photo combining the rock and a clear Fuji was found.

**9. Morito coast (森戸海岸) / Morito shrine (森戸大明神) and Manase (真名瀬), Hayama**
- GPS (approx., own estimate): rocks behind Morito shrine 35.276 N, 139.571 E; Manase bus stop approx. 35.273 N, 139.572 E.
- Motif: red torii on Najima islet ~700 m offshore plus Hayama lighthouse ("Yujiro lighthouse", built for the 3rd anniversary of actor Ishihara Yujiro's death), with Fuji behind — [tabi-mag](https://tabi-mag.jp/kn0554/)
- The view is listed as "Morito no sekisho" among Kanagawa's 50 scenic sites (Commons description) and as No. 84 of MLIT's "100 Fuji views of Kanto" — [Commons file](https://commons.wikimedia.org/wiki/File:View_of_Mount_Fuji_from_Hayama.jpg), [MLIT](https://www.ktr.mlit.go.jp/chiiki/fujimi0084.html)
- From Manase bus stop (on the seawall) the lighthouse and torii line up with Fuji above them — [tabi-mag](https://tabi-mag.jp/manase-busstop/)
- Photo: `east_morito_1.jpg` — https://commons.wikimedia.org/wiki/File:View_of_Mount_Fuji_from_Hayama.jpg — author Quercus acuta — CC0 (good: orange afterglow, Fuji silhouette, small torii and lighthouse on the rocks; telephoto).

**Backup: Koganezaki (黄金崎), Nishiizu town** — known for Suruga Bay + Fuji views and sunsets; the tip lookout is cited for its Fuji view; "Uma Rock" (horse-head rock) is the main foreground; a "sunset deck" has a board with sunset times — [tabi-mag](https://tabi-mag.jp/sz202/), [guidoor](https://www.guidoor.jp/places/532)

### Inferences
- [INFERENCE] Light direction: from West Izu (spots 1-4) Fuji lies almost due north, so in winter the low southern sun front/side-lights the snow face all day; sunrise lights it from the right (east), sunset from the left with pink alpenglow (as in `east_osezaki_2.jpg`). From Gotemba/Otome/Kintoki (6-7) Fuji is to the west-northwest: front-lit and red at sunrise, back-lit in the afternoon. From the Kanagawa coast (8-9) Fuji is to the west: front-lit but distant in the morning, silhouette at sunset.
- [INFERENCE] Focal lengths judged from the photos: Kumomi 70-135 mm (full frame) to stack rocks under Fuji; Ida 35-50 mm; Daruma ridge 24-35 mm; Kanuki-yama 24-50 mm for city + Fuji, 400 mm+ for the summit detail; Otome 50-85 mm; Morito/Manase 200-400 mm to enlarge Fuji behind torii and lighthouse.
- [INFERENCE] Least crowded: Kumomi, Ida, Osezaki outside the summer bathing/diving season, Kanuki-yama (locals only). Most "known": Daruma rest house and Otome on fine weekends; Tateishi and Morito on Diamond-Fuji evenings.

## Exact access: nearest station/bus, parking, hike time; toll roads and fees; closures

### Takeaway
West Izu is realistically a rental-car destination (buses exist but are slow and infrequent); Kanuki-yama, Otome-toge, Tateishi and Morito/Manase are easy by public transport. No toll is needed for any of the nine spots (Nishi-Izu Skyline is free).

### Cited Findings
- Kumomi: bus from Izukyu Rendaiji station to Matsuzaki bus terminal ~40 min, change to the Kumomi-Iriya bus ~20 min, stop "Kumomi-hama", 5 min walk — [iko-yo](https://iko-yo.net/facilities/27874). Parking: ~60 spaces at Kumomi port (paid) per the beach guide — [tabi-mag](https://tabi-mag.jp/kumomibeach/); a hiker describes a wide sloping parking area right at the sea that becomes paid in the summer bathing season; no convenience store nearby — [BE-PAL](https://www.bepal.net/archives/276849). No lifeguard huts/changing rooms even in season — [tabi-mag](https://tabi-mag.jp/kumomibeach/)
- Kumomi Sengen / Eboshi-yama: 130 + 320 stone steps then an unpaved path — [YAMAP](https://yamap.com/activities/21678453/article). Climb time not stated in sources.
- Kirameki-no-oka: directly on Prefectural Road 17; parking 5 cars free per tabi-mag vs 10 cars per Navitime (sources conflict) — [tabi-mag](https://tabi-mag.jp/sz464/), [Navitime](https://www.navitime.co.jp/category/0710/22203/)
- Deai-misaki: on Road 17, ~5 min by car from Heda port; parking free, "10 cars" in one source and "1 car" in another (conflict) — [nap-camp](https://www.nap-camp.com/mag/38418), [rurubu](https://rurubu.jp/andmore/spot/80022020). Shizuoka Pref. lists parking at Deai-misaki and the B&G centre — [Shizuoka Pref.](https://www.pref.shizuoka.jp/kurashikankyo/shizenkankyo/sakura/1057743/1058447/1058461.html)
- Mihama-misaki: parking 340 cars, 1,200 yen during the summer holiday period — [rurubu](https://rurubu.jp/andmore/spot/80022023)
- Osezaki: bus from Numazu station south exit (Tokai Bus, for Enashi) to the terminus ~1 h 10 min, then a shared taxi to Ose-misaki; by car ~1 h 20 min from Numazu IC via Route 414; parking ~250 cars, 310 yen/h, max 1,500 yen up to 8 h (summer rate listed elsewhere as 300 yen/h) — [rurubu](https://rurubu.jp/andmore/spot/80021999), [rurubu beach page](https://rurubu.jp/andmore/spot/80022000/beach). A summer boat sometimes runs (check) — [489ban listing](https://reserve.489ban.net/client/oosekan/0/detail/1132150)
- Daruma-yama rest house: bus from Shuzenji station (Tokai Bus for Heda) ~30 min, stop "Darumayama Kogen Rest House"; Mishima to Shuzenji by Izu-Hakone Sunzu line ~35 min; parking 50 cars free; rest house open 10:00-17:00 (Apr-Sep) / 10:00-16:30 (Oct-Mar), closed Tuesdays, cash only — [at-s](https://www.at-s.com/gourmet/article/125689), [guidoor](https://www.guidoor.jp/places/1804). Free parking and toilets at the rest house also reported by hikers — [YAMAP](https://yamap.com/activities/37278377/article)
- Nishi-Izu Skyline: toll-free — [nap-camp](https://www.nap-camp.com/mag/107165). (Do not confuse with the Izu Skyline toll road, whose tolls rose on 1 Oct 2025 — [trafficnews](https://trafficnews.jp/post/571230).)
- Kanuki-yama: 25 min walk from JR Numazu station south exit, or bus to "Kurose" stop; alternatively bus (for Sotoharanaka) to "Ryozenji", 20 min walk to Koryodai; by car ~20 min from Numazu IC / Nagaizumi-Numazu IC; free parking at Koryodai (~10 cars) and Nakase (~15 cars, 8:00-17:30); the road to Koryodai is narrow and shared with hikers, so city car parks are recommended — [walkerplus](https://koyo.walkerplus.com/detail/ar0622e154620/), [weathernews](https://weathernews.jp/koyo/spot/25032/)
- Otome-toge: bus stop "Otome-toge" beside FUJIMI CAFE GOTEMBA; the car park belongs to the cafe (use the cafe / ask before leaving a car for hiking) — [tabi-mag](https://tabi-mag.jp/otometouge/)
- Mt Kintoki: Otome-guchi bus stop to Otome-toge 40 min, Otome-toge to summit ~1 h 10 min; from Kintoki (Kintoki-jinja) shrine ~1 h 20 min to the summit; Otome-guchi car park ~60 cars, free, toilets; Kintoki trailhead car park 800 yen/day, shrine-side car park free — [san-tatsu](https://san-tatsu.jp/tabite/articles/569327/), [btimes](https://btimes.jp/shizuoka/spot/3220/), [Ministry of Environment course guide](https://www.env.go.jp/nature/nationalparks/list/fuji-hakone-izu/course/06/)
- Gotemba Heiwa Koen: ~5 min by car from Gotemba IC exit 1 towards Hakone; parking 60 cars free; listed hours 9:00-17:00; tel. 0550-82-0350 — [at-s](https://www.at-s.com/spot/article/137371)
- Tateishi Park: prefectural Tateishi car park on Route 134, 62 cars, free, with toilets; park open at all hours, free; Keikyu bus stop "Tateishi" — [tabi-mag](https://tabi-mag.jp/kn0549/), [walkerplus](https://summer.walkerplus.com/odekake/detail/ar0314s64595/data.html)
- Morito: Keikyu bus Zushi 12 (coastal route) from Zushi station to "Morito-jinja"; shrine car park 106 cars, free for 30 min for worshippers, crowded in summer — [tabi-mag](https://tabi-mag.jp/kn0554/). Manase: coastal-route Keikyu bus from Zushi station ~16 min, 3-4 buses/hour; roads narrow and little parking, bus advised — [tabi-mag](https://tabi-mag.jp/manase-busstop/), [4travel](https://4travel.jp/dm_shisetsu/11846810)
- Koganezaki (backup): bus from Shuzenji station (Tokai Bus for Matsuzaki) 1 h 14 min to "Koganezaki Crystal Park", 10 min walk; ~80 min by car from Numazu IC; parking details conflict between sources (free vs. 1,000 yen in GW/summer, 8:00-17:00) — [tabi-mag](https://tabi-mag.jp/sz202/), [nap-camp](https://www.nap-camp.com/mag/86599)
- Ashinoko Skyline (not selected): generally 7:00-19:00; toll figures conflict between sources (630 yen vs 1,000 yen for a car) — [rurubu](https://rurubu.jp/andmore/spot/80014550), [walkerplus](https://koyo.walkerplus.com/detail/ar0314e393911/data.html)

### Inferences
- [INFERENCE] A practical West-Izu loop by car from Numazu/Mishima: Osezaki, Ida (Kirameki-no-oka), Heda (Deai-misaki), up to Heda-toge and Daruma-yama, in one day; Kumomi needs an extra ~1.5 h south and is best combined with an overnight in Matsuzaki/Kumomi.
- [INFERENCE] The Daruma rest-house hours only limit the cafe; the car park terrace view is presumably accessible outside those hours [UNVERIFIED].

## Best months and time of day (winter clarity, sunset alignment / Diamond Fuji dates on the Kanagawa coast, sea of clouds, sakura)

### Takeaway
November to February is the reliable window everywhere (snow cap + dry air). Kanagawa-coast Diamond Fuji happens twice a year: early-mid April and late August-early September, on slightly different dates per beach.

### Cited Findings
- Diamond Fuji, Tateishi (Akiya/Tateishi coast): around 13-14 April and 28-29 August each year — [tabi-mag](https://tabi-mag.jp/akiya-diamondfuji/), [minnacamera](https://www.minnacamera.com/spots/1015)
- Diamond Fuji, Morito coast: early April (around 9 April) and early September (around 3 September) — [tabi-mag](https://tabi-mag.jp/kn0554/). Manase: early April and early September, dates shift slightly with leap years — [tabi-mag](https://tabi-mag.jp/manase-busstop/)
- Diamond Fuji elsewhere (not hidden spots, for reference): Inamuragasaki ~4-5 April and ~8 September; Shichirigahama ~4-5 April and ~7-8 September; Chigasaki coast ~4 April and ~7-8 September; Zushi Marina / Hiroyama Park ~5 April and ~7 September; all approximate, can shift by a day — [tabi-mag list](https://tabi-mag.jp/miuradiamondfiji/)
- Tateishi: winter gives the clearest Fuji; red Fuji and Diamond Fuji are targets; evening silhouette of the rock — [minnacamera](https://www.minnacamera.com/spots/1015)
- Gotemba Heiwa Koen sakura: usually early to mid April, Somei-yoshino first, then weeping, double-flowered and "Gotemba-zakura"; night illumination during the cherry festival (2026 festival days 4-5 April) — [jorudan](https://sp.jorudan.co.jp/hanami/spot_1225.html), [at-s event](https://www.at-s.com/event/article/119951)
- Kumomi: Fuji said to be visible behind Ushitsuki-iwa on clear days even around Obon (mid-August) — [co-trip post](https://co-trip.jp/post/400596)
- Koganezaki: crowded in cherry season, long weekends and summer — [nap-camp](https://www.nap-camp.com/mag/86599)
- General: winter's clearer skies make it the best season for Fuji; morning front light makes the scene "glow" — [Canon Snapshot](https://snapshot.asia.canon/en/article/photographing-mount-fuji-in-winter-shooting-spots-composition-tips)
