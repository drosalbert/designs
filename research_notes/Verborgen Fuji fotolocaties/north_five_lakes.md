# Hidden Mount Fuji photo viewpoints — NORTH side (Yamanashi: Fuji Five Lakes west end, Oshino, Yamanakako, Misaka range)

Research date: 20 Nov 2026. Method note: only web *search* worked in this session; direct page fetches of Japanese official sites (yamanashi-kankou.jp, fuefuki-kanko.jp, vill.oshino.lg.jp, rurubu.jp) were blocked by the network allowlist, so the facts below come from search-result extracts of those pages, not from reading the full pages. Treat exact numbers (fares, parking counts, dates) as "check before going". Photos are in `photos/` (15 files, all viewed and confirmed to show Fuji; all ≤ 1600 px, ≤ 492 KB).

Final selection: 9 spots — (1) Lake Shoji / Tatego-hama, (2) Panorama-dai above Shoji/Motosu, (3) Nakanokura-toge, (4) Ryugatake, (5) Nijumagari-toge, (6) Shindo-toge / FUJIYAMA Twin Terrace, (7) Koyodai / Sanko-dai, (8) Teppogi-no-atama (Myojin-yama), (9) Mitsutoge. Dropped: Mt. Shakushi (no usable Commons photo found, not researched further); Lake Saiko Nenba-hama (researched briefly, no Commons photo obtained — kept as a text-only bonus note).

## Which of these are actually uncrowded, and what does the shot look like?

### Takeaway
The genuinely quiet ones are those that need a walk (Panorama-dai, Nakanokura-toge, Ryugatake outside New Year, Teppogi-no-atama, Mitsutoge) and Lake Shoji; the two "terrace" spots (Nijumagari SORA no IRO, FUJIYAMA Twin Terrace) are no longer secret — they are purpose-built, promoted decks and can fill with photographers at dawn. Most north-side views look south to Fuji, so the mountain is front/side-lit at sunrise and sunset and back-lit/hazy at midday.

### Cited Findings

**1. Lake Shoji — Tatego-hama, "Kodaki Fuji" (精進湖・他手合浜 / 子抱き富士, Shōji-ko Tatego-hama)**
- From the north shore beach, the parasitic cone Omuro-yama (大室山, 1,468 m) sits in front of Fuji "as if Fuji were holding a child" — hence 子抱き富士 (Kodaki Fuji). On calm early mornings there is a mirror reflection ("sakasa Fuji"). — [nap-camp spot page](https://www.nap-camp.com/mag/spot/103353); [gltjp](https://www.gltjp.com/ja/article/item/20499/)
- Marketed by a Japanese travel site as a view with "zero man-made objects in the frame"; Lake Shoji is described as the original summer retreat discovered by an Englishman. — [tabi-mag](https://tabi-mag.jp/japan-shoji/)
- Not empty at peak times: one user report says the Tatego-hama car park was full and they had to move to the lakeshore. — [co-trip post](https://co-trip.jp/post/525121)
- Photographer etiquette posted for the spot: no long-term parking within 10 m of the waterline, no long "reserving" of tripod positions. — [minnacamera spot 1391](https://www.minnacamera.com/spots/1391)
- Photos (verified visually): `north_shojiko_1.jpg` — Fuji + Omuro-yama with soft reflection in rippled water, boats small on far shore. Commons: https://commons.wikimedia.org/wiki/File:Mount_Fuji_from_Lake_Sh%C5%8Dji_(2015-11-22).jpg — author Alpsdake — CC BY-SA 4.0. `north_shojiko_2.jpg` — tele shot, fishing boats in foreground, autumn trees, Omuro-yama in front of Fuji. Commons: https://commons.wikimedia.org/wiki/File:Mount_Fuji_and_Fishing_Boat_on_Lake_Sh%C5%8Dji.jpg — author Alpsdake — CC BY-SA 4.0.

**2. Panorama-dai above Shoji/Motosu (パノラマ台, Panorama-dai; "精進湖パノラマ台", Misaka range)**
- Viewpoint over Fuji, the Misaka and Tenshi ranges; in clear weather the southern end of the Southern Alps is visible. — [Yamanashi "Gururi Fujisan Trail" PDF rs2](https://www.yamanashi-kankou.jp/gururifujisan/documents/gft-rs2.pdf)
- From the top you see Fuji, the Aokigahara "sea of trees", Lake Motosu, Lake Shoji and the Southern Alps. — [rurubu spot page](https://rurubu.jp/andmore/spot/80018488)
- The trail is described as a well-maintained zig-zag mountain path with about 400 m of ascent; hikers call it an easy, pleasant walking path. — [wanderlog listing](https://wanderlog.com/place/details/4886163/パノラマ台-精進湖); [YAMAP report](https://yamap.com/activities/44530878/article)
- Photo (verified): `north_panoramadai_1.jpg` — wide view: Aokigahara forest plain, Mt. Ashiwada, Fuji with cloud on the right, winter scrub foreground. Commons: https://commons.wikimedia.org/wiki/File:Mount_Ashiwada_and_Mount_Fuji.jpg — author Alpsdake — CC BY-SA 4.0 (description: "seen from Panoramadai (Misaka Mountains)").

**3. Nakanokura-toge (中ノ倉峠, Nakanokura-tōge) above Lake Motosu — the 1,000-yen-note view**
- The Fuji on the 1,000-yen note is the "upside-down Fuji" from the NW shore of Lake Motosu; the actual shooting position was Nakanokura-toge, reached in about 30 min on foot from the trailhead on Prefectural Road 709. — [tabi-mag](https://tabi-mag.jp/yn0343/)
- The viewing deck is stepped in 5 tiers and holds about 30 people. — [tabi-mag](https://tabi-mag.jp/yn0343/)
- An almost identical "1,000-yen" view is available from the lakeside parking area without climbing. — [nap-camp](https://www.nap-camp.com/mag/spot/103355)
- Photo (verified, see caveat in Gaps): `north_nakanokura_1.jpg` — Lake Motosu foreground, dark flanking ridges, snow-streaked Fuji, slightly hazy. Commons: https://commons.wikimedia.org/wiki/File:Mt_Fuji.jpg — author InvictusOU812 — CC BY 2.0 (description: "taken from same spot as appears on back of 1000 yen note").

**4. Ryugatake (竜ヶ岳, Ryū-ga-take, 1,485 m) — Diamond Fuji at New Year**
- Diamond Fuji (sun rising exactly out of the summit) is visible around Ryugatake from roughly 3 Dec to 9 Jan; other reports say "mid-December to early January". — [YAMAP reports](https://yamap.com/activities/45209913/article); [YAMAP](https://yamap.com/activities/28758664/article)
- In mid-December the sun rises dead-centre from the mid-slope lookout (見晴台), not from the summit. — [YAMAP](https://yamap.com/activities/29044943/article)
- Crowding: on a weekday in Dec 2023 about 20 people at the summit and the lookout empty; on New Year's Day the lookout is reported "very crowded". — [YAMAP](https://yamap.com/activities/29044943/article); [YAMAP](https://yamap.com/activities/45181537/article)
- Photos (verified): `north_ryugatake_1.jpg` — classic Diamond Fuji, sun star on the summit, bamboo-grass (sasa) foreground. Commons: https://commons.wikimedia.org/wiki/File:Diamond_Fuji_and_sun.JPG — author Alpsdake — CC BY-SA 4.0. `north_ryugatake_2.jpg` — broad grassy summit with wooden summit post and Fuji. Commons: https://commons.wikimedia.org/wiki/File:Peak_of_Mount_Ryu_(Yamanashi).jpg — author Alpsdake — CC BY-SA 4.0.

**5. Nijumagari-toge (二十曲峠, Nijūmagari-tōge) above Oshino — terrace "SORA no IRO"**
- Viewing terrace 二十曲峠展望テラス SORA no IRO opened September 2022 at about 1,150 m in Oshino village. — [rurubu article](https://rurubu.jp/andmore/article/18355)
- Japanese blog describes it as an "anaba" (little-known) sunrise spot: Fuji turning red at dawn with sea of clouds below. — [makun-academy](https://makun-academy.com/nijyuumagari-mountain-pass-yamanashi-mtfuji/)
- Photo (verified): `north_nijumagari_1.jpg` — Fuji over the Oshino plain framed by a large red pine, fresh green, info board. Commons: https://commons.wikimedia.org/wiki/File:Mount_Fuji_from_Nijyumagari_Pass.jpg — author Hrykt — CC BY-SA 4.0.

**6. Shindo-toge (新道峠, Shindō-tōge) — FUJIYAMA Twin Terrace (FUJIYAMAツインテラス)**
- Two decks on the Misaka ridge looking straight down on Lake Kawaguchi with Fuji behind. — [Yamanashi tourism](https://www.yamanashi-kankou.jp/kankou/topics/fujiyamatwinterrace.html)
- Shooting room is tight: only about 10 people can set up cameras; in season photographers climb in the dark to shoot night view through sunrise. — [tabi-mag](https://tabi-mag.jp/yn0341/)

**7. Koyodai / Sanko-dai (紅葉台 Kōyōdai / 三湖台 Sanko-dai)**
- From Koyodai you look over Lake Motosu and Lake Saiko; Sanko-dai ("three lakes platform") is about 10 min on foot further and has the big open panorama. — [jorudan spot](https://sp.jorudan.co.jp/leaf/spot_55044.html); [YAMAP](https://yamap.com/activities/4974642/article)

**8. Teppogi-no-atama / Myojin-yama (鉄砲木ノ頭 / 明神山, 1,291 m) above Lake Yamanaka**
- The route climbs through open pampas-grass (susuki) slopes from the lakeside via the Yamanakako Panorama-dai. — [YAMAP](https://yamap.com/activities/46646793/article)
- A January 2024 report found only 3 cars at the parking area after 15:00 on a weekday. — [YAMAP](https://yamap.com/activities/29170797/article)
- Photos (verified): `north_myojinyama_1.jpg` — golden susuki slope, Lake Yamanaka, snow-capped Fuji under streaked cirrus. **9. Mitsutoge (三ツ峠, Mitsutōge; summit Kaiun-zan 1,785 m)**
- From the ridge rocks you can frame Fuji together with the Southern Alps, Lake Kawaguchi or a sea of clouds; staying at Mitsutoge-sanso hut just below the top allows night/star shots and dawn glow. — [minnacamera spot 1420](https://www.minnacamera.com/spots/1420)
- In winter the subjects are rime ice and snow-clad Fuji; mornings give the best colour, midday is hazy. — [minnacamera spot 1420](https://www.minnacamera.com/spots/1420)
- Stay on the trail: rare-plant protection areas and fenced zones are off-limits. — [minnacamera spot 1420](https://www.minnacamera.com/spots/1420)
- Photos (verified): `north_mitsutoge_1.jpg` — summit stone marker "三ツ峠", bare tree, Fuji behind. Commons: https://commons.wikimedia.org/wiki/File:Mount_Mitsutoge_(Peak).jpg — author Alpsdake — CC BY-SA 4.0. `north_mitsutoge_2.jpg` — tele shot of snow-covered Fuji over pine tops. Commons: https://commons.wikimedia.org/wiki/File:Mount_Fuji_from_Mitsutouge_(48412627661).jpg — author Raita Futo — CC BY 2.0.

**Bonus (text only, no photo): Lake Saiko Nenba-hama (西湖・根場浜)**
- NW shore of Lake Saiko; view of Fuji across the lake and over the Aokigahara forest; one of the locally chosen "Fuji Hakkei" (eight views). — [Yamanashi tourism viewpoint page](https://www.yamanashi-kankou.jp/viewpoint/pub/pvp051.html)
- A photographer site calls it a standard Fuji spot where photographers gather from early morning — i.e. not really hidden. — [minnacamera spot 1394](https://www.minnacamera.com/spots/1394)

### Inferences
- Rough crowd ranking, quietest first (my judgement from the reports above): Panorama-dai ≈ Teppogi-no-atama ≈ Nakanokura-toge deck < Ryugatake (except 31 Dec–3 Jan) < Mitsutoge < Lake Shoji < Koyodai < Nijumagari terrace < Twin Terrace in bus hours.
- Focal lengths (inferred from the downloaded photos, not from a cited source): Shoji Kodaki Fuji works at roughly 50–100 mm for Fuji + Omuro-yama, wide for reflection; Shindo-toge, Panorama-dai and Teppogi-no-atama are wide-angle scenes (about 16–35 mm); Ryugatake Diamond Fuji about 35–70 mm; Mitsutoge takes both wide (with marker stone) and tele.
- Light direction (geometry, not cited): all these spots lie N, NW or NE of Fuji. Lake Shoji, Motosu, Panorama-dai and Ryugatake are NW/W of the mountain, so the sun rises behind or beside Fuji (silhouette/Diamond Fuji in winter) and afternoon/evening gives front-lit, red "beni-Fuji". Nijumagari and Teppogi-no-atama lie NE/E, so morning light hits Fuji's face and sunset goes behind it (silhouette, evening Diamond Fuji season at Yamanakako).

## Exact access: parking, bus stop, hike duration, winter road closures, fees

### Takeaway
Shoji, Koyodai and (in season) Nijumagari are drive-up; Nakanokura is a 30-min climb, Teppogi-no-atama 20–40 min, Panorama-dai and Ryugatake are half-day hikes, Mitsutoge about 100–120 min up. The Twin Terrace is shuttle-bus-only from late April to late November (about ¥1,000–1,500 return) and walk-in only in winter; forest roads to Shindo-toge, Nijumagari and Mitsutoge trailhead close in winter, but exact 2026/27 dates were found only partially.

### Cited Findings

**Lake Shoji / Tatego-hama**
- Prefectural Shoji car park, 50 spaces, free. — [nap-camp](https://www.nap-camp.com/mag/spot/103353)
- A user report mentions a lakeshore free parking area with a ¥50 "cooperation fee". — [co-trip post](https://co-trip.jp/post/525121)
- Bus: about 40 min from Kawaguchiko Station on the sightseeing loop bus to stop 子抱富士ビューポイント (Kodaki Fuji Viewpoint); by car about 30 min from Kawaguchiko IC. — [gltjp](https://www.gltjp.com/ja/article/item/20499/)

**Panorama-dai**
- Traverse Lake Shoji west shore → Panorama-dai → Lake Motosu shore: about 2 h 30 min walking. — [rurubu](https://rurubu.jp/andmore/spot/80018488)
- One hiker: about 2 h up from the Motosu-side trailhead, about 1.5 h down to Lake Shoji. — [wanderlog](https://wanderlog.com/place/details/4886163/パノラマ台-精進湖)
- Listed as having no official parking; hikers report leaving cars on open ground at the Shoji-side trailhead (status of that land unclear). — [rurubu](https://rurubu.jp/andmore/spot/80018488); [YAMAP](https://yamap.com/activities/36350409/article)

**Nakanokura-toge**
- Trailhead on Prefectural Road 709 on the NW shore of Lake Motosu, with parking space; about 30 min on foot to the deck. — [tabi-mag](https://tabi-mag.jp/yn0343/)
- Trailhead parking described as free, about 15–20 cars; public car park near the 浩庵荘前 (Kōan-sō mae) bus stop. — [nap-camp](https://www.nap-camp.com/mag/spot/103355)
- Official trail leaflet warns the path is slippery. — [Yamanashi Gururi Fujisan Trail PDF](https://www.yamanashi-kankou.jp/gururifujisan/documents/gft-rs1.pdf)
- A 2010 Commons photo caption notes that the prefectural road beyond the 1,000-yen viewpoint was under winter closure in mid-December. — [Commons file description](https://commons.wikimedia.org/wiki/File:Mount_Fuji_and_Lake_Motosu_20101211_-_panoramio.jpg)

**Ryugatake**
- Trailhead and parking on the Lake Motosu shore near the Motosu-ko youth sports centre / Motosu-ko campsite. — [tabi-mag](https://tabi-mag.jp/ryugatake/)
- Course time: 50 min from trailhead to the rest hut/lookout near the stone Buddhas, then 1 h 20 min to the summit. — [tabi-mag](https://tabi-mag.jp/ryugatake/)
- Loop example: campsite entrance → rest hut 75 min → summit 75 min → lakeshore road 90 min → campsite entrance 10 min. — [Yamakei Online record](https://www.yamakei-online.com/cl_record/detail.php?id=51012)
- No toilets at the free hikers' car park or on the route. — [YAMAP](https://yamap.com/activities/28758664/article)
- New Year arrangement reported by a hiker: Motosu-ko campsite parking usable 30 Dec–3 Jan but toilets closed; otherwise use the Motosu-ko shop car park (public toilet), 20–30 min walk to the trailhead. — [YAMAP](https://yamap.com/activities/45178104/article)
- One report cites a notice board giving campsite winter closure from end of November to late March. — [YAMAP](https://yamap.com/activities/28758664/article)
- Pre-dawn starts around 05:30 with headlamp; sub-zero temperatures, possible ice (bring traction spikes). — [YAMAP](https://yamap.com/activities/45178104/article)

**Nijumagari-toge**
- No bus; taxi or car only. Paved but narrow forest road; microbuses and large vehicles cannot pass. — [Yamanashi Gururi Fujisan Trail PDF rs7](https://www.yamanashi-kankou.jp/gururifujisan/documents/gft-rs7.pdf); [rurubu spot](https://rurubu.jp/andmore/spot/80018339/)
- About 20 min by car from Oshino Hakkai; go up and back from the Oshino side. — [tabi-mag](https://tabi-mag.jp/yn0340/)
- Through-traffic to the Tsuru side is not possible because the road on that side is closed. — [Oshino village notice](https://www.vill.oshino.lg.jp/page/1650.html)
- The Hosono-Shishidome forest road (Tsuru side) is closed "for the time being" because of frequent rockfall. — [Yamanashi forest road info id=108](https://www.pref.yamanashi.jp/rindoujyouhou/kisei.php?id=108)
- Terrace is free to visit; "in winter access may be impossible depending on road conditions". — [rurubu spot](https://rurubu.jp/andmore/spot/80018339/)
- Parking: 18 spaces according to one listing, 10 according to another. — [rurubu spot](https://rurubu.jp/andmore/spot/80018339/); [Yamanashi viewpoint page](https://www.yamanashi-kankou.jp/viewpoint/pub/pvp055-1.html)
- A visitor report dated 11 Nov 2026 found the deck open, with frozen handrails. — [hiddenjapan-gems](https://hiddenjapan-gems.com/nijumagari-pass-mt-fuji/)

**Shindo-toge / FUJIYAMA Twin Terrace**
- 2026 season: the dedicated bus started on Mon 20 April 2026 and runs until late November; may be cancelled in bad weather. — [Fuefuki Kanko Navi](https://www.fuefuki-kanko.jp/scontents/fujiyamatwinterrace/); [Hotel Hatta notice](https://hotel-hatta.co.jp/news/1911/)
- A private guide site states 20 April–30 November 2026, 12 departures towards the terrace between 08:45 and 14:45. — [fujiyamasan.com](https://fujiyamasan.com/shindotoge/)
- Buses are EVs running every 30 min from 08:45, with a lunch break. — [rurubu article](https://rurubu.jp/andmore/article/16770)
- Fares (return): adults ¥1,000 in April, June, July, September; ¥1,500 in May, August, October, November ("best season"); children ¥500 / ¥750. — [rurubu article](https://rurubu.jp/andmore/article/16770); [fujiyamasan.com](https://fujiyamasan.com/shindotoge/)
- Tickets are sold at the tourist base "Lily Bell Hütte" (Suzuran-no-sato, Ashigawa) and can also be bought in the Jorudan "Norikae Annai" app. — [Fuefuki Kanko Navi](https://www.fuefuki-kanko.jp/scontents/fujiyamatwinterrace/)
- Winter: because the forest road is closed, there is no shuttle bus from late November to late April. — [Yamanashi tourism spot page](https://www.yamanashi-kankou.jp/kankou/spot/fujiyamatsuinterasu.html)
- Walking alternative: about 40 min on a mountain path from the Suzuran (lily-of-the-valley) colony car park; beware snow and ice. Visitor parking: Suzuran-no-sato "Sawazuma-tei" (15 cars, closed Tue) and Ashigawa farm shop "Ogossō-ya" (25 cars, closed Tue). — [Yamanashi tourism spot page](https://www.yamanashi-kankou.jp/kankou/spot/fujiyamatsuinterasu.html)
- The Mizugasawa forest road (水ヶ沢林道) is closed in the snow season, mid-December to late April; then it is about 40 min on foot from the forest road entrance to the pass. — [tabi-mag](https://tabi-mag.jp/yn0341/)

**Koyodai / Sanko-dai**
- Bus: Kawaguchiko Station → Fujikyu bus towards Lake Motosu, 22–25 min to 紅葉台入口 (Kōyōdai-iriguchi), then 20–25 min on foot. — [walkerplus](https://koyo.walkerplus.com/detail/ar0419e12605/); [jorudan](https://sp.jorudan.co.jp/leaf/spot_55044.html)
- Car: 11 km from Kawaguchiko IC on Route 139 towards Saiko/Motosu; free parking, "use the car park at the foot"; a free car park with toilet is mentioned. — [walkerplus](https://koyo.walkerplus.com/detail/ar0419e12605/); [YAMAP](https://yamap.com/activities/4974642/article)
- The access track to Koyodai is unpaved for a bit over 1 km. — [YAMAP](https://yamap.com/activities/4974642/article)
- Walking the area is free; the rooftop deck of the Koyodai rest house costs ¥300 adults / ¥200 children, open 08:30–17:00; the rest house closes on snowy days from late January to early March. — [rurubu spot](https://rurubu.jp/andmore/spot/80018403/koyo); [yukoyuko](https://www.yukoyuko.net/onsen/spot/s0002272)

**Teppogi-no-atama (Myojin-yama)**
- About 20 min from the Mikuni-toge (三国峠) parking area/trailhead to the summit; no toilet there. — [YAMAP](https://yamap.com/activities/47291023/article)
- From Yamanakako Koryu Plaza "Kirara": about 30 min up through susuki fields to the Panorama-dai, then on to the summit; Kirara car park was ¥300 (April 2026 report), with office and toilets. — [YAMAP](https://yamap.com/activities/46646793/article); [YAMAP](https://yamap.com/activities/48725536/article)
- Panorama-dai ↔ summit return recorded at 1 h 04 min. — [YAMAP](https://yamap.com/activities/4621287)
- A March 2026 report says a pay car park accepts coins only and neither new nor old types — bring suitable coins. — [YAMAP](https://yamap.com/activities/46623810/article)
- On Yamanakako Road Race day (May) the car parks around the lake are reserved for the event. — [YAMAP](https://yamap.com/activities/48725536/article)

**Mitsutoge**
- By car: Route 137 from Ichinomiya-Misaka IC, turn onto the old Misaka road (Prefectural Road 708) before the New Misaka Tunnel to the Mitsutoge trailhead car park (unmarked roadside spaces). — [Yamakei Online record](https://www.yamakei-online.com/cl_record/detail.php?id=84517)

## Best months and time of day

### Takeaway
Late autumn to winter (November–February) is the prime window almost everywhere: clear air, snow cap, autumn colour in mid-November, and Diamond Fuji at Ryugatake from early December to early January. Dawn is best for reflections (Shoji, Motosu), sea of clouds (Nijumagari, Shindo-toge) and Diamond Fuji; late afternoon suits the susuki slopes above Yamanakako.

### Cited Findings
- Lake Shoji: mirror reflection appears on calm early mornings. — [nap-camp](https://www.nap-camp.com/mag/spot/103353)
- Ryugatake Diamond Fuji: about 3 Dec–9 Jan; mid-December best from the mid-slope lookout, New Year from the summit area. — [YAMAP](https://yamap.com/activities/45209913/article); [YAMAP](https://yamap.com/activities/29044943/article)
- Fujikawaguchiko tourism info gives photo times between 06:30 and 07:52 for end of December at Ryugatake. — [Yamanashi Diamond Fuji page, Ryugatake](https://www.yamanashi-kankou.jp/fujisan/tanosimo/daiamond_fuji/ryugatake.html)
- No-hike alternative for New Year Diamond Fuji: Ryujin-ike pond at Fuji Motosuko Resort had special early opening 27 Dec 2025–4 Jan 2026, with sunrise over Fuji at about 07:30–08:00. — [tabi-mag](https://tabi-mag.jp/ryujinpond/)
- Nijumagari-toge: sunrise, with Fuji turning red and sea of clouds. — [makun-academy](https://makun-academy.com/nijyuumagari-mountain-pass-yamanashi-mtfuji/)
- Shindo-toge: night view through sunrise is what photographers go for; the winter ridge is praised for star shots, sub-zero sea of clouds and twilight gradients. — [tabi-mag](https://tabi-mag.jp/yn0341/); [minnacamera spot 1397](https://www.minnacamera.com/spots/1397)
- Koyodai autumn colour: forecast peak from about 13 November, leaf fall from about 29 November. — [weathernews](https://weathernews.jp/koyo/spot/24905/)
- Mitsutoge: morning sky colours on the Fuji side; midday haze reduces clarity, so start early or stay overnight; winter gives rime ice. — [minnacamera spot 1420](https://www.minnacamera.com/spots/1420)
- Teppogi-no-atama: snow still lying around the area in March. — [YAMAP](https://yamap.com/activities/46623810/article)
- Photo evidence of season: the Commons shots of Lake Shoji (22 Nov 2015) and Teppogi-no-atama (21 Nov 2015) show late-November conditions — brown/golden grass, light snow cap. — [Commons](https://commons.wikimedia.org/wiki/File:Mount_Fuji_from_Lake_Sh%C5%8Dji_(2015-11-22).jpg); [Commons](https://commons.wikimedia.org/wiki/File:Mount_Fuji_and_Lake_Yamanaka_(2015-11-21).jpg)

### Inferences
- Sea of clouds at Nijumagari and Shindo-toge is most likely on cold, still mornings in autumn and early winter (general meteorology; the sources mention the phenomenon but give no months).
- Teppogi-no-atama's golden susuki is an October–November subject (matches the 21 Nov photo); late afternoon side-light on the grass is the natural choice.

### Gaps
- No published 2026/27 Diamond Fuji date/time table for Ryugatake was retrieved (the official Yamanashi page exists but could not be opened). The exact New Year's Day time is unconfirmed.
- No sourced "best month" statement for Panorama-dai, Nakanokura-toge or Lake Shoji beyond the general winter-clarity pattern.

## Recent changes 2024–2026: restrictions, closures, new terraces, reservation systems

### Takeaway
The biggest current item is Mitsutoge: part of the Mitsutoge footpath has been closed since 29 June 2026 after earthquake rockfall, with no reopening date found. Otherwise the changes are infrastructure-driven: the Twin Terrace is now a managed, paid-shuttle site (5th-anniversary fares in 2026, app ticketing), and Nijumagari got its SORA no IRO terrace in 2022, with the Tsuru-side approach closed.

### Cited Findings
- Mitsutoge: because of rockfall from the earthquake of Fri 26 June 2026, a section of the Mitsutoge footpath is closed from 17:00 on Mon 29 June 2026 "for the time being"; section: Haha-no-shirataki waterfall (母ノ白滝) to the junction of prefectural forest road Nishikawa-Arakura line and Shirataki forest road. It stays closed until countermeasure works are finished; hikers are asked to use other routes; reopening to be announced on the prefectural website. — [Yamanashi Prefecture press PDF](https://www.pref.yamanashi.jp/documents/126704/2606mitsutouge.pdf)
- Twin Terrace: 2026 is promoted as the 5th anniversary with special fares (¥1,000 normal months / ¥1,500 best-season months). — [rurubu article](https://rurubu.jp/andmore/article/16770)
- Twin Terrace: tickets newly purchasable in the Jorudan "Norikae Annai" app; cancellations announced on Fuefuki Kanko Navi and the terrace's Instagram. — [Fuefuki Kanko Navi](https://www.fuefuki-kanko.jp/scontents/fujiyamatwinterrace/); [Fuefuki city](https://www.city.fuefuki.yamanashi.jp/kanko/sports/spot/fujiyamatwinterrace.html)
- Nijumagari: terrace SORA no IRO opened September 2022; Oshino village publishes a dedicated "cautions when visiting the terrace" page; no through-route to Tsuru. — [rurubu article](https://rurubu.jp/andmore/article/18355); [Oshino village](https://www.vill.oshino.lg.jp/page/1650.html)
- Nakanokura-toge: now has a 5-tier viewing deck for about 30 people. — [tabi-mag](https://tabi-mag.jp/yn0343/)

### Inferences
- The Mitsutoge closure section (Haha-no-shirataki side) is on the Kawaguchiko/Kawaguchi-Asama-shrine approach, so the standard short route from the Misaka-side Mitsutoge trailhead (Pref. Road 708) appears unaffected — but that is my reading of the place names, not stated in the source; and that road is itself winter-closed.
- None of the nine spots appears to have an entry reservation system; the only paid, capacity-managed access is the Twin Terrace shuttle.
