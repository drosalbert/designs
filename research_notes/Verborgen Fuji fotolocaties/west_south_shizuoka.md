# Hidden Mount Fuji viewpoints — West & South side, Shizuoka Prefecture (Fujinomiya, Asagiri, Fuji City, Shimizu coast)

Researcher's notes, status 20 November 2026.

## Key question 1 — Which spots are actually uncrowded, and what does the shot look like?

### Takeaway
Of the candidates, the genuinely quiet ones are Yamamiya Sengen-jinja, the Fuji River Shinkansen levee, Tagonoura Minato Park, Iwamotoyama Park outside blossom weeks and Lake Tanuki outside the two Diamond-Fuji weeks; Obuchi Sasaba is no longer a secret (50,000+ visitors a year, foreign tour groups, manner problems reported June 2026), and Satta-toge and Ryuganbuchi are small sites that fill up on clear days or in bloom.

### Cited Findings

**Spot A — Lake Tanuki / 田貫湖 (Tanuki-ko), Fujinomiya** — GPS approx. 35.344 N, 138.558 E (west shore, deck in front of Kyukamura Fuji; unverified)
- Composition (from the photos checked): Fuji due east across a small lake, clean reflection on windless mornings; the Osawa-kuzure gully faces the camera. Wide to standard lens.
- The site is extremely crowded only in the two Diamond-Fuji weeks: "April and August, about a week each, very crowded", with car parks reported full from the middle of the night — [fujisantotomoni.jp](https://fujisantotomoni.jp/spot/%E7%94%B0%E8%B2%AB%E6%B9%96)
- One listing describes it as a quiet, little-visited lake outside those periods — [rtrp.jp](https://rtrp.jp/locations/1330/scenes/14/)
- Photos:
  - `west_tanukiko_1.jpg` — https://commons.wikimedia.org/wiki/File:Tanuki_ko_no_Fuji_san_20181130.jpg — AnthonyKwok — CC BY 4.0 (elevated view, lake inlet, reflection, 30 Nov 2018)
  - `west_tanukiko_2.jpg` — https://commons.wikimedia.org/wiki/File:Mount_Fuji_from_Laku_Tanuki.JPG — Alpsdake — CC BY-SA 4.0 (shore level, evening red light on the snow cap)

**Spot B — Asagiri Highlands / 朝霧高原 (Asagiri Kogen) incl. Fumotoppara / ふもとっぱら, Fujinomiya** — GPS approx. Michi-no-eki Asagiri Kogen 35.413 N, 138.592 E; Fumotoppara 35.400 N, 138.565 E (both unverified)
- Asagiri has no single "standard" point: few buildings, lots of pasture, Fuji visible from almost everywhere — [fujisantotomoni.jp](https://fujisantotomoni.jp/spot/%E6%9C%9D%E9%9C%A7%E9%AB%98%E5%8E%9F)
- Named points: observation deck behind Michi-no-eki Asagiri Kogen (grass in front of Fuji); "Asagiri Sawayaka Parking", a free lay-by on Route 139 facing Fuji; Asagiri Arena nature park (low-angle lawn shots) — [4travel](https://4travel.jp/dm_shisetsu/11347898), [matsukiyo.cafe](https://matsukiyo.cafe/asagirikogen/)
- The road from Route 139 towards Asagiri Arena, "a road leading to Fuji", is recommended as a photo spot by the Fujinomiya tourism association — [fujinomiya.gr.jp](https://fujinomiya.gr.jp/winter_fujisan/)
- Light: one guide says the west face is front-lit in the late afternoon and can turn red at sunset (single, weakly supported source) — [fujiyamasan.com](https://fujiyamasan.com/asagiri-kogen/)
- Fumotoppara: huge meadow campsite; a pond near "Kanayama Terrace" gives a reflection when there is no wind — [fujiyamasan.com](https://fujiyamasan.com/fumotoppara/)
- Photos:
  - `west_asagiri_1.jpg` — https://commons.wikimedia.org/wiki/File:Michinoeki_Asagiri_Kogen.JPG — Alpsdake — CC BY-SA 4.0 (roadside station with Fuji behind; documentary rather than scenic)
  - `west_fumotoppara_1.jpg` — https://commons.wikimedia.org/wiki/File:2018-03-18_The_camper_at_the_foot_of_Mt._Fuji.jpg — irisgazer — Public domain (meadow, tents, full west face)

**Spot C — Yamamiya Sengen-jinja / 山宮浅間神社, Fujinomiya** — GPS approx. 35.271 N, 138.637 E (unverified)
- A shrine with no main hall: a worship platform (遥拝所, yohaijo) is laid out facing Fuji; reached by a flight of steps; Fuji visible "weather permitting" — [hotokami.jp](https://hotokami.jp/area/shizuoka/Hmmty/Hmmtytk/Dassk/144752/), [Shizuoka Prefecture cultural properties](https://www.pref.shizuoka.jp/kankosports/bunkageijutsu/bunkazai/1002825/1041003/1041889/1004987/1021569.html)
- Visitor reviews report both a cap cloud on Fuji and complete cloud cover; no source describes crowds — [4travel](https://4travel.jp/dm_shisetsu/11369803)
- Photo: none kept. The only Commons image of the platform (File:Yamamiya-sengen-jinja, youhaijo-1.jpg, CC0) was downloaded and checked: it shows the stone enclosure and trees but no Fuji, so it was deleted.

**Spot D — Obuchi Sasaba / 大淵笹場, Fuji City** — address 富士市大淵1445; GPS approx. 35.209 N, 138.697 E (unverified)
- One of the few places where Fuji can be shot over tea fields with no power lines; the local preservation society had poles and frost fans removed — [Fuji City](https://www.city.fuji.shizuoka.jp/sp/machi/c0504/rn2ola000000dvpa.html), [thelocality.net](https://thelocality.net/obuchisasaba/)
- Not uncrowded any more: more than 50,000 visitors a year — [Shizuoka Prefecture magazine](https://fmc.pref.shizuoka.jp/fujinokuni/number_60/7927/); TV reports in 2026 speak of a surge of foreign visitors driven by the matcha boom — [khb-tv](https://www.khb-tv.co.jp/news/16542808)
- Composition (from the photos checked): terraced tea rows in the foreground, a small red-roofed hut and two solitary trees in the middle, Fuji with Mt. Hoei on the right shoulder. Standard to short telephoto.
- Photos:
  - `west_obuchi-sasaba_1.jpg` — https://commons.wikimedia.org/wiki/File:Obuchi_Sasaba.jpg — SHori — CC BY 4.0 (fresh green tea, dawn light)
  - `west_obuchi-sasaba_2.jpg` — https://commons.wikimedia.org/wiki/File:MtFuji_FUjiCity_Obuchi.jpg — Mocchy — CC BY-SA 4.0 (autumn, deep-blue sky)

**Spot E — Imamiya tea fields / 今宮の茶畑, Fuji City** — GPS: not found; located a short walk from the "Imamiya" (今宮) junction of prefectural roads 24 and 76
- Tea rows with Fuji and no visible poles or wires; less developed than Obuchi Sasaba, with no official facilities — [pixpot.net](https://www.pixpot.net/view_spots/spot/1804/imamiya-chabatake-fuji), [matsukiyo.cafe](https://matsukiyo.cafe/imamiyanochabatake/)
- Weekday early mornings are reported as the way to avoid other photographers — [merci-nagoya.jp](https://merci-nagoya.jp/fuji-tea-field/)
- Photo: none found on Commons (the search API was heavily rate-limited; two attempts failed).

**Spot F — Satta-toge / 薩埵峠 (Satta Pass), Shimizu-ku, Shizuoka City** — GPS approx. 35.080 N, 138.541 E (observation deck; unverified)
- Composition (from the photo checked): Fuji above Suruga Bay, with the Tomei Expressway, Route 1 and the Tokaido main line curving along the shore below; the Hiroshige view. About 50 mm full-frame equivalent is the suggested focal length; a polarising filter is recommended — [matsukiyo.cafe](https://matsukiyo.cafe/sattatoge/)
- Popular for night shots with car light trails — [select333.com](https://select333.com/satta-touge-access/)
- Crowding: after reopening in January 2025 it "became a topic on social media" and tourists and amateur photographers come mainly at weekends — [Shizuoka Shimbun](https://news.at-s.com/article/1656363); the small car park fills on days when Fuji is visible — [shifu-dsuki.com](https://shifu-dsuki.com/sathutatougefujisan.html)
- Photos:

**Spot G — Ryuganbuchi / 龍巌淵, Fuji City (Urui River / 潤井川)** — GPS approx. 35.184 N, 138.638 E (unverified)
- "Clear Urui River, Fuji and cherry blossom in one frame"; the rows of trees stand between Ryugan bridge (龍巌橋) and Takido bridge (滝戸橋); the best shooting position is said to be on the opposite (left) bank — [Weathernews](https://weathernews.jp/sakura/spot/50026/), [pixpot.net](https://www.pixpot.net/articles/u_d_view/244)
- Not quiet in bloom: rated "crowded on weekdays, very crowded on holidays" — [Weathernews](https://weathernews.jp/sakura/spot/50026/)

**Spot H — Iwamotoyama Park / 岩本山公園, Fuji City** — GPS approx. 35.180 N, 138.621 E (unverified)
- About 320 plum trees and 500 cherry trees; Fuji framed by blossom; the observation deck also overlooks Suruga Bay and the Ashitaka mountains — [Fuji City](https://www.city.fuji.shizuoka.jp/1005330000/miryoku/p007535.html), [fujisan-kyokai.jp](https://www.fujisan-kyokai.jp/viewspot50/359)

**Spot I — Fuji River Shinkansen bridge / 富士川橋梁 (Fujikawa kyoryo), Fuji City** — GPS approx. 35.143 N, 138.623 E (right/west bank levee; unverified)
- Shot from the levee road and the riverbed on the right bank, near the Fujikawa Ryokuchi baseball ground; from close by you get "Fuji under the bridge, train on top" — [rokko1gophoto](https://rokko1gophoto.hateblo.jp/entry/2024/09/26/231648), [pixpot.net](https://www.pixpot.net/view_spots/spot/2498%20/fujigawa-bridge/)
- Light: afternoon is front-lit for trains heading towards Osaka — [rokko1gophoto](https://rokko1gophoto.hateblo.jp/entry/2024/09/26/231648)
- Composition (from the photos checked): steel truss bridge with a white N700 across the lower third, the whole cone above; works both as a wide panorama and as a telephoto compression.
- Photos:

**Spot J — Tagonoura / 田子の浦: Fujinokuni Tagonoura Minato Park (ふじのくに田子の浦みなと公園), Fuji City** — GPS approx. 35.138 N, 138.697 E (unverified)
- The park's "Fujisan Dragon Tower" lookout gives Fuji over the working port and Suruga Bay; twilight shots combine Fuji with the industrial night lights; known locally as a first-sunrise spot — [yakei.jp](https://yakei.jp/japan/spot.php?i=tagonoura), [Fuji City](https://www.city.fuji.shizuoka.jp/1005330000/miryoku/p007434.html)
- Composition (from the photos checked): harbour basin, ships, red-and-white chimneys with steam, the full south face with Mt. Hoei on the right.
- Photos:
  - `west_tagonoura_1.jpg` — https://commons.wikimedia.org/wiki/File:Mount_Fuji_from_Fujisandoragon_tower.jpg — Alpsdake — CC BY-SA 4.0 (from the Dragon Tower)

**Spot K — Nihondaira / 日本平, Shizuoka City (borderline: a known viewpoint, not hidden)** — GPS approx. 34.972 N, 138.467 E (unverified)
- Listed among local residents' recommended Fuji viewpoints; also named as a tea-field-plus-Fuji location — [Hello Navi Shizuoka](https://hellonavi.jp/feature/fuji-view-spots), [pixpot.net](https://www.pixpot.net/articles/u_d_view/334/fuji-chabatake/)
- Photo: `west_nihondaira_1.jpg` — https://commons.wikimedia.org/wiki/File:Mount_Fuji_and_Port_of_Shimizu_from_Nihondaira.jpg — Alpsdake — CC BY-SA 4.0 (Fuji above Shimizu port and the bay)

### Inferences
- Ranking by "hidden" value for a foreign visitor: Yamamiya Sengen-jinja, Fuji River levee, Tagonoura and Imamiya are the least visited; Lake Tanuki and Asagiri are quiet most of the year but famous among Japanese photographers; Obuchi Sasaba and Satta-toge are now on tour itineraries and should be presented as "less visited than Kawaguchiko, but not empty".
- All these spots look at the south or west face, so Mt. Hoei's bump shows on the right-hand slope from the south (Obuchi, Tagonoura, Fuji River), and the mountain is back-lit at sunrise from the west (Tanuki, Asagiri), which is exactly what makes Diamond Fuji possible there.
- Nihondaira is better dropped or mentioned only in passing; it does not fit "hidden".

### Gaps
- No source found describing actual visitor numbers at Yamamiya, Imamiya, Tagonoura or the Fuji River levee; "uncrowded" for those rests on the absence of crowd reports.
- Shimizu port / S-Pulse Dream Plaza: not researched (ran out of budget); no notes, no photo.
- No verified GPS for any spot; no location at all for the exact Imamiya shooting position.
- No Commons photo for Imamiya; no Fuji-view photo for Yamamiya; only one, loosely labelled, photo for Ryuganbuchi.

## Key question 2 — Exact access, parking, fees and current closures (late 2026)

### Takeaway
Satta-toge is open: the walking trail closed by a June 2022 collapse reopened on 22 January 2025. Almost every spot is free; the practical bottleneck is parking (Satta about 8 spaces, Ryuganbuchi and Imamiya none official) and thin bus timetables in Fujinomiya.

### Cited Findings
- **Satta-toge status:** Shizuoka City's hiking page states restoration is complete and the route has been passable since Wednesday 22 January 2025 — [Shizuoka City](https://www.city.shizuoka.lg.jp/s5423/s012474.html)
- **Satta-toge cause:** in June 2022 soil near the observation deck collapsed in heavy rainy-season rain, the path and handrail subsided and up to about 700 m was closed; the prefecture confirmed safety in mid-January 2025 and removed the barriers ("first time in two and a half years") — [Shizuoka Shimbun](https://news.at-s.com/article/1656363)
- **Satta-toge caveat:** a travel site warns that works or heavy rain may bring new restrictions and asks visitors to check beforehand — [toretabi.jp](https://www.toretabi.jp/zipangclub/page/tabineta2510_3.html). A Shizuoka Shimbun headline reports a Golden Week closure of the Route 1 Fuji-Yui bypass near Satta-toge after part of a slope face fell onto the road (year not confirmed from the snippet; this concerns the highway below, not the trail) — [Shizuoka Shimbun](https://news.at-s.com/article/1961260)
- **Satta-toge access:** free car park at the deck for about 8 cars (sources say 7 to 10), no large vehicles, plus about 4 spaces on the Okitsu side; toilet at the car park; 2 minutes on foot to the deck; JR Yui station about 50 minutes on foot, JR Okitsu about 45 to 50 minutes; article said to be checked October 2026 — [fujiyamasan.com](https://fujiyamasan.com/satta-pass/)
- **Satta-toge road:** the approach is so narrow two cars can barely pass; bring a torch at night; no convenience store nearby — [pixpot.net](https://www.pixpot.net/articles/u_d_view/209/satta-toge-access), [sakrutch.com](https://sakrutch.com/2022_shizuoka_nightview_sattatouge/)
- **Lake Tanuki:** free parking, about 200 spaces on the south side (buses allowed) and about 40 on the north side (another source says about 30; a third says 250 in total); 5 to 10 minutes on the lakeside path from the north car park; bus from JR Fujinomiya bound for Inokashira (猪之頭), 45 minutes to "Kyukamura Fuji" (休暇村富士), few departures — [fujisantotomoni.jp](https://fujisantotomoni.jp/spot/%E7%94%B0%E8%B2%AB%E6%B9%96), [rurubu](https://rurubu.jp/andmore/spot/80022235), [fujisan-kyokai.jp](https://www.fujisan-kyokai.jp/viewspot50/345)
- **Asagiri Kogen:** Fujikyu Shizuoka bus from JR Fujinomiya (towards Fujisan station via Lake Motosu), about 40 to 50 minutes to "Asagiri Kogen"; limited timetable; by car 35 minutes from Shin-Fuji IC (Shin-Tomei) or 40 from Fuji IC (Tomei) — [Hello Navi Shizuoka](https://hellonavi.jp/detail/page/detail/1168), [fujiyama-navi.jp](https://www.fujiyama-navi.jp/entries/doESt)
- **Fumotoppara:** reservation only, through the official website; bookings open at 19:00 on the first Wednesday of the month for the month after next — [fujiyamasan.com](https://fujiyamasan.com/fumotoppara/). A camper's report dated October 2026 gives 1,000 yen per person (junior-high age and up), 500 yen per primary-school child and 2,000 yen per car per night — [dpar72.com](https://dpar72.com/2026/10/21/post-35423)
- **Yamamiya Sengen-jinja:** grounds open at all times, free; information centre open Saturdays, Sundays and holidays 10:00 to 15:00 only (tel. 0544-58-5190); free parking for about 10 cars (another source: 10 + 5) — [rurubu](https://rurubu.jp/andmore/spot/80043699), [Shizuoka Prefecture](https://www.pref.shizuoka.jp/kankosports/bunkageijutsu/bunkazai/1002825/1041003/1041889/1004987/1021569.html)
- **Yamamiya by bus:** Fujinomiya City says bus from JR Fujinomiya stop 4 (Awakura-Manno line) to "Manno Danchi Iriguchi", about 9 minutes, 250 yen, then about 30 minutes on foot — [Fujinomiya City](https://www.city.fujinomiya.lg.jp/1015150000/p001614.html); contradicted by a private guide that says Fujikyu bus towards Shiraito Falls to "Yamamiya" stop, then 10 minutes on foot — [shrine-access.com](https://shrine-access.com/yamamiya-sengen/). By car about 15 minutes from Fujinomiya station.
- **Obuchi Sasaba:** entry and parking free; car park 1 has 25 standard + 3 light-car + 1 accessible spaces, car park 2 has 2 car and 2 coach spaces (coaches must ask the Fujisan Tourism and Exchange Bureau first, 0545-64-3776); set the sat-nav to "Kyu Fujita-tei" (旧藤田邸) for car park 2, car park 1 lies further in, about 300 m from the shooting area; about 10 minutes from Shin-Fuji IC — [Fuji City](https://www.city.fuji.shizuoka.jp/sp/machi/c0504/rn2ola000000dvpa.html), [fuji-fujinomiya.goguynet.jp](https://fuji-fujinomiya.goguynet.jp/2024/05/02/sasaba-3/)
- **Imamiya:** no official car park. One guide recommends parking at "Imamiya Fureai Koen" (今宮ふれあい公園) and walking about 10 minutes; another mentions an informal space east of the junction used by visitors; do not stop on the shoulder of the main road — [matsukiyo.cafe](https://matsukiyo.cafe/imamiyanochabatake/), [chikyudori.com](https://www.chikyudori.com/japan/green-tea-field/)
- **Ryuganbuchi:** Fuji City lists parking as "none" and recommends the train: JR Minobu line, Iriyamase station (入山瀬駅), about 10 minutes on foot — [Fuji City](https://www.city.fuji.shizuoka.jp/1005330000/miryoku/p008193.html); one blog says 20 minutes. In the 2026 season temporary car parks were set up, for example east of Takido bridge near the water shrine, already 60 to 70 percent full on a weekday morning; temporary toilets provided; the bridge is one car wide — [fujichari.jp](https://fujichari.jp/ryuganbuchi-season/)
- **Iwamotoyama Park:** free parking for roughly 290 cars (sources: 286, 293, 300); car parks 1 and 2 open 8:30 to 21:00, car park 3 open 24 hours; community bus "Koume" from JR Fuji station, about 35 to 40 minutes, not running on Sundays and holidays; 20 minutes by car from Fuji IC — [Fuji City](https://www.city.fuji.shizuoka.jp/1040200000/shisetsu/p000062.html), [rurubu](https://rurubu.jp/andmore/spot/80022478)
- **Fuji River bridge:** JR Fujikawa station (Tokaido main line), 15 to 20 minutes on foot to the levee; about 3.5 km or 7 minutes from Fujikawa smart IC — [surugawan.net](https://www.surugawan.net/guide/556.html), [rokko1gophoto](https://rokko1gophoto.hateblo.jp/entry/2024/09/26/231648). Parking: one blog says the riverbed "Ikoi no Hiroba" car park takes about 50 cars — [makun-academy.com](https://makun-academy.com/fujigawabridge/); contradicted by the Fuji tourism guide which lists "parking: none" — [surugawan.net](https://www.surugawan.net/guide/556.html)
- **Tagonoura Minato Park:** park open all day, free; car park reported open 8:00 to 17:00 (one source 8:30; a night-view site says closed at 19:00), gate closes at 17:00; 3 minutes from car park to tower; JR Yoshiwara station about 25 minutes on foot; 15 to 20 minutes by car from Fuji IC or Shin-Fuji IC — [yakei-fan.com](https://yakei-fan.com/yakei/tokai/shizuoka/tagonoura-minato-park.html), [aihana-travel.com](https://aihana-travel.com/tagonoura-minato-park), [yakei.jp](https://yakei.jp/japan/spot.php?i=tagonoura)

### Inferences
- Without a car, the realistic rail-based spots are Ryuganbuchi (Iriyamase), the Fuji River levee (Fujikawa), Satta-toge (Yui or Okitsu, a 45 to 50 minute uphill walk each way) and Tagonoura (Yoshiwara). Tanuki, Asagiri, Yamamiya, Obuchi and Imamiya effectively need a car or taxi.
- Because the Tagonoura car park closes around 17:00, sunset and blue-hour shooting there means arriving on foot or accepting that the car must be outside the gate.
- Satta-toge should be described as "open since January 2025, but a landslide-prone slope: check Shizuoka City's hiking page the day before".

### Gaps
- Could not open Shizuoka City's page directly to confirm that no new Satta-toge restriction was posted in autumn 2026; the "open" status rests on the city page snippet and a guide said to be updated October 2026.
- No bus route found to Obuchi Sasaba or Imamiya; no bus timetable times for Tanuki or Asagiri.
- Fuji River levee parking is contradictory (about 50 spaces versus none).
- Current Fumotoppara prices are from one camper's blog, not the official site; day-visit (non-camping) entry rules for photographers not found.
- Winter road conditions (ice, snow) for Asagiri, Tanuki and Yamamiya: no information found.

## Key question 3 — Best months and time of day

### Takeaway
Fixed dates worth planning around: Lake Tanuki Double Diamond Fuji about 20 April and 20 August (about a week each, around 06:00), Fumotoppara Diamond Fuji in early March and early October, fresh tea green mid-April to mid-May, Ryuganbuchi and Iwamotoyama cherry late March to early April, Iwamotoyama plum early February to early March; winter gives the clearest air everywhere.

### Cited Findings
- **Tanuki Double Diamond:** visible twice a year, about a week around 20 April and around 20 August, at about 06:00 — [gurutabi.gnavi.co.jp](https://gurutabi.gnavi.co.jp/a/a_1976/), [fujisan-kyokai.jp](https://www.fujisan-kyokai.jp/viewspot50/345)
- **Tanuki detail:** in April the sun rises about 06:00, in August about 06:08; around 20 August the alignment falls on the middle of the west shore and the point shifts from south to north day by day; at the deck in front of Kyukamura Fuji the alignment is about 24 April and about 19 August — [shizuoka-hamamatsu-izu.com](https://shizuoka-hamamatsu-izu.com/shizuoka/fujinomiya-city/double-diamondfuji/), [fujiyamasan.com](https://fujiyamasan.com/tanukiko/)
- **Tanuki conditions:** needs a clear, windless morning; any breeze ruffles the lake and destroys the reflection — [trip.iko-yo.net](https://trip.iko-yo.net/articles/4508)
- **Timing conflict:** one blog says it takes roughly an hour from sunrise time until the sun reaches the summit — [matrix-travel.net](https://matrix-travel.net/tanukiko); this sits awkwardly with "around 06:00" from the other sources, so the exact minute is unresolved.
- **Fumotoppara Diamond Fuji:** the first few days of March and of October are "dead centre"; those nights are hard to book — [happycamper.jp](https://happycamper.jp/_ct/17532563/p3/), [dpar72.com](https://dpar72.com/2026/10/21/post-35423)
- **Tea green:** Obuchi Sasaba at its best from mid-April to early May (another source: to mid-May); a local writer says Golden Week is the peak — [traveljapan47.net](https://traveljapan47.net/archives/2433/), [Yahoo creators](https://creators.yahoo.co.jp/akepon/0100219720). Imamiya: mid-April to mid-May — [merci-nagoya.jp](https://merci-nagoya.jp/fuji-tea-field/)
- **Obuchi tea festival:** "Obuchi Ocha Matsuri" each May with a photo session (tea-picker costumes) and tea picking; held on 3 May in 2024; very crowded that day — [fuji-fujinomiya.goguynet.jp](https://fuji-fujinomiya.goguynet.jp/2024/05/02/sasaba-3/), [Fuji City](https://www.city.fuji.shizuoka.jp/1035200000/ocha/p007899.html)
- **Ryuganbuchi cherry:** normally late March to early April; in 2026 the trees were about half open on 24 to 27 March; clear mornings recommended; lit up at night — [Weathernews](https://weathernews.jp/sakura/spot/50026/), [fujichari.jp](https://fujichari.jp/ryuganbuchi-season/)
- **Iwamotoyama:** plum early February to early March, cherry late March to early April; the 2026 "Zekkei Fujisan Marugoto Iwamotoyama" event ran 1 February to 10 April — [Fuji City](https://www.city.fuji.shizuoka.jp/1005330000/miryoku/p007528.html), [at-s.com](https://www.at-s.com/event/article/119399)
- **Satta-toge:** winter, when the air is clear; just before sunset or early morning; one photographer who arrived an hour before sunrise found it already quite light — [ameblo kei7474](https://ameblo.jp/kei7474/entry-12334038777.html), [chikyudori.com](http://www.chikyudori.com/japan/satta-touge/)

- **Winter in Fujinomiya:** the Fujinomiya tourism association runs a dedicated page of winter Fuji photo spots, naming Lake Tanuki's Diamond Fuji and the Asagiri area — [fujinomiya.gr.jp](https://fujinomiya.gr.jp/winter_fujisan/)

### Inferences
- A late-April trip is the strongest single window for this side of the mountain: Tanuki Double Diamond (about 20 to 25 April) and fresh tea at Obuchi/Imamiya overlap, and the snow cap is still large.
- From the south-side spots (Obuchi, Imamiya, Tagonoura, Fuji River, Satta) Fuji lies to the north or north-east, so the mountain is side-lit morning and evening and never strongly back-lit; mornings are safer simply because cloud builds on the south slope during the day (geometry and common experience, not stated in a source).
- The 20 April / 20 August dates are approximate "about a week" windows; nobody should promise a single day.

## Key question 4 — Recent changes 2024–2026 (restrictions, closures, parking rules)

### Takeaway
Two real changes: Satta-toge reopened in January 2025 after two and a half years, and Obuchi Sasaba started asking for a 100-yen cooperation fee in April 2026 after repeated trespassing, unauthorised drones, graffiti and litter. No ban on photographers was found anywhere.

### Cited Findings
- **Obuchi Sasaba problems:** Yomiuri TV reported on 2 June 2026 that unauthorised drone flights, people walking into the fields, graffiti and littering keep occurring; locals remove graffiti with a pressure washer or grind it off the concrete with a deck brush, several times over the past one to two years — [Yomiuri TV via Yahoo News](https://news.yahoo.co.jp/articles/37ca9b0cb5084f22b5a2c6d627ed305a2e7a2134)
- **Obuchi Sasaba fee:** entry and parking remain free, but since April 2026 tour operators are asked for 100 yen per person, used for upkeep and staffing — [Yomiuri TV via Yahoo News](https://news.yahoo.co.jp/articles/37ca9b0cb5084f22b5a2c6d627ed305a2e7a2134). The site's own page says every guided tour, large or small, pays 100 yen per person, individual visitors are asked to contribute voluntarily, guides must e-mail in advance, and the land is private, not a public facility; managed by the Obuchi 2-chome Sasaba Landscape Preservation Society (大淵二丁目ささば景観保存会) — [sasaba-ohbuchi.com](https://sasaba-ohbuchi.com/)
- **Obuchi Sasaba rules:** Fuji City asks visitors not to enter the tea fields, to use the photographers' car park only, to obey the one-way farm road, drive slowly and take rubbish home — [Fuji City](https://www.city.fuji.shizuoka.jp/sp/machi/c0504/rn2ola000000dvpa.html)
- **Obuchi Sasaba tone:** the society's head says nuisance behaviour is unacceptable but many travellers mean no harm, and the fee is requested "while keeping a spirit of hospitality" — [Yomiuri TV via Yahoo News](https://news.yahoo.co.jp/articles/37ca9b0cb5084f22b5a2c6d627ed305a2e7a2134)
- **Imamiya:** entering the tea fields is prohibited as a rule — [akistyle.jp](https://akistyle.jp/obuchisasaba-imamiya-fujisan)
- **Satta-toge:** closed June 2022, reopened 22 January 2025 (details under Key question 2) — [Shizuoka City](https://www.city.shizuoka.lg.jp/s5423/s012474.html)
- **Ryuganbuchi:** traffic restrictions are applied on weekends during the expected bloom; the on-site park car park cannot be used and drivers are directed to temporary car parks — [Fuji City](https://www.city.fuji.shizuoka.jp/1005330000/miryoku/p008193.html), [happy-cielo.com](https://happy-cielo.com/archives/report/%E5%AF%8C%E5%A3%AB%E5%B1%B1%E3%81%AE%E3%83%91%E3%83%AF%E3%83%BC%E3%82%92%E6%84%9F%E3%81%98%E3%82%8B%E6%9C%80%E9%AB%98%E3%81%AE%E3%81%8A%E8%8A%B1%E8%A6%8B%E3%82%92%EF%BC%81-%E3%80%8C%E9%BE%8D%E5%B7%8C/)
- **Lake Tanuki campsite:** from stays on 1 August 2026 (Reiwa 8) the campsite restricts group camping; a Lake Tanuki festival with fireworks is listed for 5 December 2026 with parking permits for campers — [nap-camp.com](https://www.nap-camp.com/shizuoka/11868/topics), [hanabi.cloud](https://hanabi.cloud/html/Tohkai/shizuoka/2026/2026-12-05_tanukiko-matsuri.html)
