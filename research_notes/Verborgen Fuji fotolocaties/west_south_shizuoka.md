## Spots with photos so far (minimum data)

### 1. Lake Tanuki — 田貫湖 (Tanuki-ko), Fujinomiya
- GPS (approx., unverified): 35.3440 N, 138.5585 E (west-shore deck in front of Kyukamura Fuji)
- Composition: Fuji due east across the lake, reflection ("sakasa Fuji"); Double Diamond Fuji at sunrise around 20 April and 20 August (about one week each, approx. 06:00). Very crowded in those weeks, quiet otherwise.
- Access: bus from JR Fujinomiya (bound for Inokashira) ~45 min to "Kyukamura Fuji"; free parking (south ~200, north ~40; sources differ).
- Photos:

  - `west_tanukiko_2.jpg` — https://commons.wikimedia.org/wiki/File:Mount_Fuji_from_Laku_Tanuki.JPG — Alpsdake — CC BY-SA 4.0

### 2. Fumotoppara — ふもとっぱら, Asagiri Highlands (朝霧高原), Fujinomiya
- GPS (approx., unverified): 35.3995 N, 138.5650 E
- Composition: huge open meadow with full west face of Fuji, small pond for reflection; Diamond Fuji early March and early October (per camper blogs). Reservation-only campsite, paid entry.
- Photo: `west_fumotoppara_1.jpg` — https://commons.wikimedia.org/wiki/File:2018-03-18_The_camper_at_the_foot_of_Mt._Fuji.jpg — irisgazer — Public domain

### 3. Yamamiya Sengen-jinja — 山宮浅間神社, Fujinomiya
- GPS (approx., unverified): 35.2713 N, 138.6368 E
- Composition: shrine without main hall; stone worship platform (遥拝所) aligned on Fuji through cedars. Grounds open at all times, free; info centre weekends/holidays 10:00-15:00; free parking ~10-15 cars.
- Photo: `west_yamamiya-sengen_1.jpg` — https://commons.wikimedia.org/wiki/File:Yamamiya-sengen-jinja,_youhaijo-1.jpg — Saigen Jiro — CC0 (to be visually checked)

### 4. Obuchi Sasaba — 大淵笹場, Fuji City
- GPS (approx., unverified): 35.2090 N, 138.6965 E; address 富士市大淵1445
- Composition: tea rows leading to Fuji, no power lines. Best mid-April to early/mid May. Private land: do not enter fields; 100 yen/person cooperation fee asked of guided tours since April 2026, voluntary for individuals. Free parking (25+3+1 and a second lot).
- Photos:
  - `west_obuchi-sasaba_1.jpg` — https://commons.wikimedia.org/wiki/File:Obuchi_Sasaba.jpg — SHori — CC BY 4.0
  - `west_obuchi-sasaba_2.jpg` — https://commons.wikimedia.org/wiki/File:MtFuji_FUjiCity_Obuchi.jpg — Mocchy — CC BY-SA 4.0

### 5. Satta-toge — 薩埵峠, Shimizu-ku, Shizuoka City
- GPS (approx., unverified): 35.0800 N, 138.5415 E (observation deck)
- Composition: Fuji over Suruga Bay with Tomei expressway, Route 1 and Tokaido line curving below (Hiroshige view). Trail reopened 22 January 2025 after closure since June 2022. ~8 free parking spaces, narrow road; 45-50 min walk from JR Yui or JR Okitsu. Best in winter, early morning or dusk.
- Photos:
  - `west_satta-toge_1.jpg` — https://commons.wikimedia.org/wiki/File:Mount_Fuji_from_Satta_Pass.jpg — Alpsdake — CC BY-SA 4.0
  - `west_satta-toge_2.jpg` — https://commons.wikimedia.org/wiki/File:Morgenrot_of_Mount_Fuji_from_Satta_Pass.jpg — Alpsdake — CC BY-SA 4.0

### 6. Ryuganbuchi — 龍巌淵, Fuji City (Urui River)
- GPS (approx., unverified): 35.1840 N, 138.6380 E
- Composition: cherry trees along the Urui river with Fuji behind; late March to early April, morning light. No official parking; JR Iriyamase station ~10 min walk (one source says 20). Crowded in bloom.
- Photo: `west_ryuganbuchi_1.jpg` — https://commons.wikimedia.org/wiki/File:Ryuganbuchi.jpg — Sablier de Verrie — CC BY-SA 4.0 (taken 8 March 2020, before bloom; to be visually checked)

### 7. Iwamotoyama Park — 岩本山公園, Fuji City
- GPS (approx., unverified): 35.1797 N, 138.6212 E
- Composition: plum (early Feb to early Mar) or cherry (late Mar to early Apr) framing Fuji. ~290 free parking spaces; community bus "Koume" from JR Fuji ~35-40 min (no Sundays/holidays).
- Photo: `west_iwamotoyama_1.jpg` — https://commons.wikimedia.org/wiki/File:MtFuji_IwamotoPark.jpg — Mocchy — Public domain

### 8. Fujikawa bridge Shinkansen shot — 富士川橋梁, Fuji City
- GPS (approx., unverified): 35.1430 N, 138.6230 E (right/west bank levee)
- Composition: Tokaido Shinkansen crossing the Fuji River with Fuji above. JR Fujikawa station 15-20 min walk to the levee.
- Photos:

### 9. Nihondaira — 日本平, Shizuoka City (borderline: well known)
- GPS (approx., unverified): 34.9725 N, 138.4672 E
- Composition: Fuji above Shimizu port and Suruga Bay.
- Photo: `west_nihondaira_1.jpg` — https://commons.wikimedia.org/wiki/File:Mount_Fuji_and_Port_of_Shimizu_from_Nihondaira.jpg — Alpsdake — CC BY-SA 4.0
