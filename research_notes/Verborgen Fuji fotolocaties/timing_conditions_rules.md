## 1. Visibility statistics: how often and when is Fuji visible?

### Takeaway
Fuji is visible mostly from November to February and rarely from June to August; early morning (around 8:00) is clearly the best time and visibility drops through the afternoon. From the south (Fuji City) the whole mountain was visible at 8:00 on 136 days in 2025 (37%); from Tokyo, only about 95-135 days per year, concentrated in December-January.

### Cited Findings
South side (Fuji City, Shizuoka - official observation from city hall, three times daily 8:00 / 12:00 / 16:00, running since 1990/1991):
- 2025 (Reiwa 7): whole mountain visible at 8:00 on 136 days (37.3%); visible in full at all three observation times on only 62 days — [Fuji City "Fujisan observation record 2025" PDF](https://www.city.fuji.shizuoka.jp/documents/7545/fujisan-kiroku2025.pdf)
- 2025 monthly "whole mountain visible at 8:00" days as read from the (garbled) PDF table by the search tool: Jan 19, Feb 22, Mar 13, Apr 9, May 9, Jun 2, Jul 5, Aug 3, Sep 6, Oct 10, Nov 19, Dec 19 (sums to 136; individual months are "snippet only", verify in the PDF) — [Fuji City PDF 2025](https://www.city.fuji.shizuoka.jp/documents/7545/fujisan-kiroku2025.pdf)
- 2024: whole mountain visible at 8:00 on 150 days; best months December 27 days (87.1%, a record), January 23, March 15; worst month August with 4 days. Fully visible at all three times (8/12/16h) on 61 days; not visible at all on 75 days — [Shizuoka Shimbun](https://news.at-s.com/article/1659200); [Fujisan Keizai Shimbun](https://mtfuji.keizai.biz/headline/2648/)
- Other annual totals of "whole mountain visible" days: 2018 = 98 (record low), 2019 = 119, 2020 = 126, 2021 = 162, 2023 = 154 — [Fujisan Keizai 2019](https://mtfuji.keizai.biz/headline/1416/); [2020](https://mtfuji.keizai.biz/headline/1668/); [2021](https://mtfuji.keizai.biz/headline/1909/); [2023](https://mtfuji.keizai.biz/headline/2403/)
- Long-term pattern (35 years, 1991-2025): most visible days November-February, fewest June-August; the share of "whole mountain visible" is highest at 8:00 and falls towards the afternoon. Clear weather does not guarantee a visible Fuji ("sunny-day rate and visibility rate are not proportional"); spring haze reduces visibility — [Fuji City PDF 2025](https://www.city.fuji.shizuoka.jp/documents/7545/fujisan-kiroku2025.pdf); [older Fuji City PDF](https://www.city.fuji.shizuoka.jp/fujijikan/fujipedia/kb719c0000000fmc-att/dual7g000000014g.pdf)

Long distance (Tokyo):
- Tokyo Metropolitan Government observes from the 31st floor of the No. 2 Government Building at 9:00 each morning. Average since FY1992: 95.2 days/year; FY2023-2024: roughly 115-135 days; 1971-1991 average was only 40.7 days — [Tokyo Metropolitan Government, "days Fuji was visible"](https://www.taiki.kankyo.metro.tokyo.lg.jp/taikikankyo/fuji/fuji_nisu.html); summarised at [fuji55.sakura.ne.jp](https://fuji55.sakura.ne.jp/index_siryo/009mieru/mieru.html)
- Tokyo monthly averages (Tocho data, FY1992-2014): January 19.1 days, December 18.1, November 11.3, August 1.7. Only December and January average 15+ days (snippet only) — [1manken blog analysis of Tocho data](https://1manken.hatenablog.com/entry/2015/12/21/060054); [fuji55.sakura.ne.jp](https://fuji55.sakura.ne.jp/index_siryo/009mieru/mieru.html)
- Private observatory in Tokyo (the Seikei school weather station in Musashino, per Nikkei): Fuji visible from Tokyo on 138 days in 2014, about 6 times the figure of 50 years earlier, attributed to cleaner air and drier urban air — [Nikkei](https://www.nikkei.com/article/DGKKZO82644330R30C15A1CC0000/); [Yahoo/Morita Masamitsu](https://news.yahoo.co.jp/expert/articles/b251d2d751e046f06063f70203752c2c25a819fc)
- The Seikei station has recorded visibility targets every morning at 9:00 since 1963 — [Tenki (Meteorological Society of Japan) 1994](https://www.metsoc.jp/tenki/pdf/1994/1994_10_0711.pdf)

Change in the data series:
- A 2026 JMA/Kofu document notes that staff visual observation ended with 2025 (the 2026 cold season) (snippet only; it is not fully clear whether this refers to Fuji City's observation or JMA's own visual snow observations) — [JMA Kofu PDF](https://www.data.jma.go.jp/kofu/image/chousa/hatsukansetsu2026.pdf)

### Inferences
- Planning rule: Nov-Feb gives roughly 2 in 3 mornings with a fully visible Fuji from the south (19-27 days per month in recent data); Jun-Aug gives roughly 1 in 10 (2-5 days per month). A summer trip needs many buffer days; a winter trip of 3-4 days almost always yields at least one clear morning.
- Only about 45% of the "clear at 8:00" days stay clear all day (62 of 136 in 2025; 61 of 150 in 2024), so shoot at dawn and treat afternoons/sunsets as a bonus, especially outside winter.
- Tokyo figures (about 95-135 days) vs Fuji City (136-162 days) show long-distance views lose a further share of days to haze; long-distance shots are essentially a Dec-Jan morning business.

### Gaps
- No north-side (Fujiyoshida / Kawaguchiko / Yamanakako) long-term visibility statistic was found. The comparison "north vs south" could not be quantified. General knowledge (unsourced here): in summer the south flank clouds over earlier in the day due to moist sea air; in winter the north side is clear and dry but colder.
- Monthly multi-year averages for Fuji City (not just 2024/2025) are in the PDF but could not be read.
- Seikei's own current figures and the 2025 Tokyo figures could not be retrieved.

## 2. Diamond Fuji and Pearl Fuji calendar, and tools to compute exact dates

### Takeaway
Diamond Fuji is fully predictable: sunset versions at Lake Yamanaka run mid-October to end-February (location shifts along the shore week by week), sunrise versions at Lake Tanuki around 20 April and 20 August, and at Ryugatake/Lake Motosu around New Year. Tokyo and the Chiba/Kanagawa coast get it twice a year on fixed dates. Exact dates shift 1-2 days by viewpoint and leap-year cycle, so use a calculator.

### Cited Findings
Lake Yamanaka (sunset):
- Visible from the Yamanakako lakeshore from mid-October to late/end February, with the point changing by season — [Yamanashi official tourism](https://www.yamanashi-kankou.jp/special/diamond_fuji.html); [Yamanakako village official page](https://www.vill.yamanakako.yamanashi.jp/holiday_diamondfuji.php)
- By point (official/regional tourism sites): Panorama-dai (Mikuni pass road) and Asahigaoka lakeside park: mid-late October and mid-late February; Hirano shore / Kirara: late October and mid-February; Nagaike Shinsui Park: early November and early February; Hananomiyako Park: about late November to mid-January (about 1.5 months) — [Yamanashi official tourism](https://www.yamanashi-kankou.jp/special/diamond_fuji.html); [Fuji,CanGo](https://www.fuji-net.co.jp/report/cat-view-camera/5126/); [Fuji,CanGo winter](https://www.fuji-net.co.jp/report/cat-view-camera/1759/)
- Example times from the tourism association: Panorama-dai area 16:22 on 16 October; Asahigaoka shore 16:08 on 18 October. Sunset contact times across the season fall between about 15:23 and 16:50; on 22 February 2026 it was about 16:34 — [Fuji,CanGo](https://www.fuji-net.co.jp/report/cat-view-camera/5126/); [watabi.org guide](https://watabi.org/articles/yamanakako-diamond-fuji-guide)
- The village brands 1-22 February each year as "Yamanakako DIAMOND FUJI WEEKS"; the Ice Candle Festival was held at Kirara on 22 Feb 2026. 2027 dates not yet announced (pattern suggests 1-22 Feb 2027) — [tabi-mag](https://tabi-mag.jp/diamond-fuji-weeks/); [tabi-mag ice candle](https://tabi-mag.jp/yamanaka-icecandle/)
- Caveat from one guide: October-December weather is less stable, February is more reliable — [watabi.org guide](https://watabi.org/articles/yamanakako-diamond-fuji-guide)
- The Yamanakako tourism association publishes an official "Diamond Fuji guide map" with dates/times per point, and a live camera — [Fuji,CanGo](https://www.fuji-net.co.jp/report/cat-view-camera/5126/)

Lake Tanuki (sunrise, with reflection = "double diamond"):
- Around 20 April and around 20 August, about one week of early mornings each; the date differs by about 4 days between the north car park pier, the deck in front of Kyukamura Fuji and the south-shore campsite terrace — [Kyukamura press release](https://www.qkamura.or.jp/qkamura/data/press_data/pdf/press_pdf_1_20230411_125537.pdf); [tabi-mag](https://tabi-mag.jp/sz070/)
- Field report: Diamond Fuji with reflection seen from Chojagatake above Lake Tanuki on 18 Aug 2025 — [YAMAP report](https://yamap.com/activities/42293737/article)

Ryugatake / Lake Motosu (sunrise, around New Year):
- Ryugatake summit: Diamond Fuji at sunrise on 1 January (2026: sunrise 6:47, free, requires a night hike) — [Rurubu](https://rurubu.jp/andmore/spot/20003958); [tabi-mag](https://tabi-mag.jp/ryugatake/)
- Ryujin pond (Fuji Motosuko Resort, the shibazakura site): double Diamond Fuji in early December and early January. The resort ran a paid "Diamond Fuji viewing" 27 Dec 2025 - 4 Jan 2026, 7:00-9:00 (entry from 2:00 on 1 Jan), adults 1,000 yen. 2026/27 edition not yet confirmed — [tabi-mag](https://tabi-mag.jp/ryujinpond/); [Fuji Motosuko Resort](https://www.fujimotosuko-resort.jp/diamond_fuji/index.html); [press release](https://san-tatsu.jp/pressrelease/488411/)
- Fujikawa town (Takaori district, Yamanashi, west of Fuji): sunrise from the summit from about 20 December to 3 January (winter solstice to New Year) — [MLIT Kanto "Fujimi 100" no. 112](https://www.ktr.mlit.go.jp/chiiki/fujimi0112.html)

Tokyo / Chiba / Kanagawa (sunset; dates from MLIT Kanto Regional Bureau list, computed with Kashmir 3D; 1-2 days variation by leap-year cycle):
- Nippori Fujimizaka (Arakawa-ku, Tokyo): around 30 January and around 11 November — [MLIT Kanto Diamond Fuji list](https://www.ktr.mlit.go.jp/chiiki/chiiki00000111.html)
- Tateyama, Chiba: Hojo beach 13-14 May and 29-30 July; Shiroyama Park 16-17 May and 27 July; Ito 29-30 May and 13-14 July (all sunset) — [MLIT Kanto Diamond Fuji list](https://www.ktr.mlit.go.jp/chiiki/chiiki00000111.html)
- Tokyo Tower: twice a year, February and November; 2026: about 17:03 on 9 February and about 16:33 on 2 November — [Tokyo Tower](https://www.tokyotower.co.jp/event/DFuji2026.11/)
- Tokyo Skytree: event on 8 November 2026 — [Enjoy Tokyo](https://www.enjoytokyo.jp/event/2004310/)
- Sunshine 60 observatory: viewing event 25-29 January 2026 — [ReseMom](https://resemom.jp/article/2025/12/24/84441.html)
- Tama Monorail publishes a station-by-station Diamond Fuji table each season (2025-2026 edition online) — [Tama Monorail](https://www.tama-monorail.co.jp/guide/diamond-fuji-2025-2026.html)

Pearl Fuji (moon on the summit):
- Kyukamura Fuji (Lake Tanuki) promotes "Moonlight Fuji" and "Pearl Fuji" from late October to about February, when air is clearest — [haveagood-holiday](https://haveagood-holiday.com/ko/articles/kyukamura-fuji-moonlight-pearl-fuji-autumn-winter-2026)

Tools named in sources:
- MLIT Kanto Regional Bureau "Kanto Fujimi 100" Diamond Fuji pages (definition + list of 128 views / 233 points, dates simulated with Kashmir GIS software) — [MLIT definition](https://www.ktr.mlit.go.jp/chiiki/chiiki00000112.html); [MLIT list](https://www.ktr.mlit.go.jp/chiiki/chiiki00000111.html)
- Yamanakako village Diamond Fuji page and tourism association guide map — [Yamanakako village](https://www.vill.yamanakako.yamanashi.jp/holiday_diamondfuji.php)
- Overview of when/where — [tenki.jp column, Jan 2025](https://tenki.jp/suppl/k_mochizuki/2025/01/21/32581.html)

### Inferences
- For a late Nov 2026 - Feb 2027 trip, the sunset Diamond Fuji is at Hananomiyako Park (late Nov - mid Jan), sunrise double diamond at Ryujin pond / Ryugatake (early Dec - early Jan), then the Yamanakako shore sequence in February (Nagaike early Feb, Hirano/Kirara mid Feb, Asahigaoka and Panorama-dai mid-late Feb). April 2027 and August 2027: Lake Tanuki around the 20th.
- Tokyo dates repeat almost exactly each year (early Nov and early Feb for central Tokyo towers; late Jan for Ikebukuro/Nippori), so the 2026 dates are a good proxy for 2027: Tokyo Tower around 9 Feb 2027, Sunshine 60 around 25-29 Jan 2027.
- Pearl Fuji needs a near-full moon rising/setting on the summit azimuth; there is no fixed annual calendar, it must be computed per viewpoint.

### Gaps
- No day-by-day official 2026/27 Yamanakako calendar could be opened; get the tourism association guide map on site or online.
- No 2026/27 Pearl Fuji date list found. Not verified in this research but widely used by photographers for exact computation: PhotoPills, The Photographer's Ephemeris, Suncalc/Mooncalc, Kashmir 3D, and Japanese Diamond/Pearl Fuji calculator sites. These could not be source-checked here.
- Kanagawa coast dates (e.g. Enoshima/Shonan, typically early April and early September in general knowledge) and Mt Takao (around winter solstice in general knowledge) were not confirmed by a source in this session.
- Lake Motosu shoreline dates other than the resort/Ryugatake were not found.

## 3. Seasonal phenomena

### Takeaway
First snow cap for this season was declared on 30 October 2026 (28 days later than normal, new measuring method). Beni-Fuji (pink snow) is the winter phenomenon (Nov-Apr), Aka-Fuji (red bare rock) the late-summer one (late Jul - early Sep, minutes after sunrise). Sea of clouds peaks Sep-Nov at dawn after rain; reflections need no wind; cap clouds signal strengthening wind.

### Cited Findings
First snow cap (hatsukansetsu):
- 2026: JMA announced Fuji's first snow cap on Friday 30 October 2026 — 28 days later than the normal date (2 October) and 7 days later than 2025 (23 October). Summit temperature was below -5 C — [tenki.jp, 30 Oct 2026](https://tenki.jp/forecaster/deskpart/2026/10/30/41146.html); [Weathernews](https://weathernews.jp/news/202610/300081/)
- Method change: from this season (cold season 2027) the century-old visual observation from Kofu was abolished; first snow cap is now determined from "analysed snow depth" (AMeDAS, radar, Himawari satellite), which also works at night/under cloud. Dates are not directly comparable with earlier years — [Weathernews](https://weathernews.jp/news/202610/300081/); [CNA](https://cna.com.tw/news/aopl/202610040134.aspx)
- Reference: 2025 first snow cap 23 October (21 days later than normal); latest ever 7 November 2024; normal date 2 October — [JMA Kofu 2025 PDF](https://www.jma-net.go.jp/kofu/image/2025/20251023_fujihatukansetu.pdf); [JMA records](https://www.data.jma.go.jp/stats/data/mdrr/fsn_cap/records/325.html); [CNA 2024](https://www.cna.com.tw/news/aopl/202410280345.aspx)
- The Fuji Subaru Line was fully closed by snow on 30 Oct 2026 and reopened fully on 2 Nov 2026 — [colorfulrock](https://colorfulrock.com/yamanobori/fuji-subaru-line-closure-2026/)

Aka-Fuji vs Beni-Fuji:
- Beni-Fuji: snow-covered Fuji turning pink in low sun, snow season about November-April. Aka-Fuji: snow-free slopes glowing red-brown, about July-October — [Fujisan-net (Yamanashi Nichinichi)](https://www.fujisan-net.jp/post_detail/2001831); [efujisan Beni-Fuji guide 2026](https://efujisan.com/fuji_point/beni_fuji.html)
- Aka-Fuji practical window: late July to early September, just after sunrise about 5:00-5:30, lasting only a few minutes to 10 minutes; needs a clear eastern horizon, viewpoint east/north-east of Fuji, best after rain — [koriotsu](https://koriotsu.com/akafuji/); [oriorihakken](https://oriorihakken.com/2110.html)

Sea of clouds (unkai):
- Conditions: clear calm night with radiative cooling, high low-level humidity (rain the previous day), weak wind, day-night temperature difference of 10 C or more, an inversion layer; a migratory high pressure the morning after rain gives large unkai — [Yamareco](https://www.yamareco.com/modules/diary/170189-detail-314746); [yume-tabi](https://yume-tabi.info/articles/yun-haigafa-shengsurutiao-jiantoqi/); [blog analysis](https://ameblo.jp/satoagg/entry-12093803029.html)
- Season: autumn to early spring, best September-November; around Fuji chances rise from autumn to early winter. Timing: before dawn to early morning, mostly gone within 2-3 hours after sunrise — [unkai guide 2026](https://xn--29sob207cg49a.com/calendar/unkai-sea-of-clouds-guide-2026/); [fujisan-nature](https://fujisan-nature.com/blog/2017/10/09/%E5%AF%8C%E5%A3%AB%E5%B1%B1%E3%81%AE%E9%9B%B2%E6%B5%B7/)
- Rain the day before is not sufficient if the air mass turns dry — [blog analysis](https://ameblo.jp/satoagg/entry-12093803029.html)

Cap clouds, lenticulars, reflections:
- Kasagumo (cap cloud) and tsurushi-gumo (lenticular) depend on the vertical profile of wind and humidity; tsurushi-gumo is caused mainly by mountain waves. Both often precede strengthening wind. Sakasa-Fuji (reflection) and kearashi (lake steam fog) require windless conditions (source is a general explainer blog) — [hedgehog.ryukyu](https://hedgehog.ryukyu/fujisan-mezurasi-gensyou/)
- tenki.jp has a column on reading Fuji's clouds (condensation and convection) — [tenki.jp](https://tenki.jp/suppl/miyata_y/2021/06/25/30469.html)

Summer night: climber lights and Milky Way:
- Official climbing season about early July to 10 September; mid-July to mid-August is the peak for lines of climber headlamps, sunrise and unkai. In 2026 the Subaru Line private-car restriction ran 3 July 18:00 - 10 September 18:00 (shuttle bus only) — [Kawaguchiko.net](https://kawaguchiko.net/fujisubaruline/); [Yamanashi flyer 2026](https://www.pref.yamanashi.jp/documents/94367/flyer_mycar2026.pdf)
- Milky Way season about June-October; darkest lakes are Motosu, Saiko and Shoji (least light pollution) — [stargazing guide 2026](https://xn--29sob207cg49a.com/calendar/fuji-lakes-stargazing-guide-2026/)

Flowers:
- Fuji Shibazakura Festival 2026: 11 April - 24 May, 8:00-16:00; usual peak late April - early May. 2027 listed as "early April - late May", not confirmed by the organiser — [Yamanashi official tourism](https://www.yamanashi-kankou.jp/kankou/spot/p2_4916.html); [Japan Travel](https://en.japantravel.com/yamanashi/fuji-shibazakura-festival/13035)
- Arakurayama Sengen Park cherry season traffic-guard period in 2026 was 1-17 April (indicative of the bloom window) — [Fujiyoshida sakura season info](https://fujiyoshida.net/en/highlight/sakuraseason_info)

### Inferences
- With the snow cap arriving late (30 Oct 2026; 23 Oct 2025; 7 Nov 2024 — three late years in a row against a 2 October norm), October is no longer a safe month for a "white-capped Fuji with autumn colour"; mid-November onwards is.
- A photographer arriving late November 2026 is in Beni-Fuji season: aim at the snow face at sunrise from the east/north-east (Yamanakako, Oshino side) and sunset glow from the west.
- For 2027 the 2026 climbing-season dates (early Jul - 10 Sep) are the best proxy for headlamp trails; the 2027 season is not yet announced.

### Gaps
- No sourced statistics found for kasagumo frequency by month or for wind thresholds for reflections (general knowledge, unsourced: reflections are most reliable at dawn before thermal wind starts; cap clouds are most frequent in the cold half-year and with an approaching front/low).
- Not source-verified in this session: windows for sakura around the lakes (generally early-mid April), fresh green tea fields at Obuchi Sasaba (generally late April - early May), susuki (generally October), autumn colour at Kawaguchiko (generally early-mid November; the 2026 festival dates were not retrieved).
- Milky Way direction relative to Fuji: sources contradict each other (one says south-side lakes align best because the galactic core is in the south; the same source lists north-side lakes as classics). Geometry suggests: from north-side lakes Fuji lies to the south, so the summer core does stand near Fuji — verify with PhotoPills/Stellarium per viewpoint.

## 5. Restrictions, overtourism measures and etiquette (2024-2026)

### Takeaway
The Fuji area is actively managing photo crowds: the Lawson Kawaguchiko barrier came back in a lower form in August 2025, Fujiyoshida cancelled its 2026 cherry blossom festival and uses guards and timed rotation at the Chureito deck, the Yume no Ohashi bridge got fencing, and drones are restricted by a patchwork of aviation law, landowner bans and municipal park bans. No new entry fee for viewpoints was found; the fees that exist are parking fees and the 4,000-yen climbing fee.

### Cited Findings
Lawson Kawaguchiko Station-front:
- May 2024: Fujikawaguchiko town installed a black mesh screen 2.5 m high and 20 m long on the pavement opposite the store after jaywalking, littering and trespassing; at least 10 holes were poked in it within days — [AP via KSAT](https://www.ksat.com/gallery/news/2024/04/30/to-fend-off-tourists-a-town-in-japan-is-building-a-big-screen-blocking-the-view-of-mount-fuji); [AFP](https://www.afpbb.com/articles/-/3522013)
- August 2024: screen taken down ahead of Typhoon Ampil and not put back, with the warning that it would return if problems resurfaced — [Time Out Tokyo, Aug 2024](https://www.timeout.com/tokyo/news/mt-fuji-photo-block-at-kawaguchiko-lawson-store-has-been-removed-082224)
- 7 August 2025: a new barrier went up — a brown tarp only about 1.4 m high, which keeps people out of the road but still allows photos over it (Time Out citing the Mainichi) — [Time Out Tokyo, Aug 2025](https://www.timeout.com/tokyo/news/the-mt-fuji-barrier-at-lawson-in-kawaguchiko-is-back-up-again-081525)
- Crowds have moved to other Lawson branches with a Fuji view — [Tokyo Weekender](https://www.tokyoweekender.com/japan-life/things-to-do-in-tokyo/tourists-flock-to-alternative-mount-fuji-lawson/)

Fujisan Yume no Ohashi (Fuji City, Shizuoka):
- 2024: after tourists climbed over the half-wall into traffic and photographed from the roadway, barricades were installed and a 1.8 m metal mesh fence was announced for end June/July 2024 — [Time Out Tokyo, Jun 2024](https://www.timeout.com/tokyo/news/another-barricade-has-been-installed-near-mt-fuji-to-prevent-unruly-tourist-behaviour-060324); [Mothership](https://mothership.sg/2024/06/anti-tourist-fence-mount-fuiji-bridge/); [Malay Mail](https://malaymail.com/news/life/2024/06/09/japan-to-build-anti-tourist-fence-at-mount-fuji-viewpoint/138917)
- Fuji City runs a standing "council on coexistence of tourism and community" for the bridge, with the national road administrator — [Fuji City document](https://www.city.fuji.shizuoka.jp/documents/16071/2-1.pdf)
- From 8 January 2026: experimental digital audio map via QR codes at the bridge car park and walkway, and a signed "Yume no Ohashi Heart Route" to spread visitors to nearby sights — [Fuji City document](https://www.city.fuji.shizuoka.jp/documents/15017/2.pdf)
- Visitor rules in guides: stay on the pavement/stairs, never enter the roadway or median, no entry to private land, park only in the small designated lot (about 10-17 cars, one-way entrance on the east side). No photography ban as such — [GLTJP](https://www.gltjp.com/en/directory/item/18437/); [Lens Tokyo](https://www.lenstokyo.com/locations/fujisan-yume-no-ohashi-bridge)

Fujiyoshida (Honcho street, Arakurayama Sengen Park / Chureito):
- 3 February 2026: the city cancelled the Arakurayama Sengen Park cherry blossom festival (about 200,000 visitors, up to 10,000/day) citing overtourism: litter, traffic, trespassing; mayor Horiuchi: "Mount Fuji is not just a tourist attraction; it is our very way of life". The park itself stayed open — [Euronews](https://www.euronews.com/2026/02/06/our-quiet-lives-are-threatened-mount-fuji-cherry-blossom-festival-axed-over-bad-tourist-be); [Time Out](https://www.timeout.com/asia/news/why-this-japanese-city-cancelled-its-famous-cherry-blossom-festival-022026); [SoraNews24](https://soranews24.com/2026/02/05/japans-most-famous-mt-fuji-view-park-cancels-cherry-blossom-festival-because-of-overtourism/amp/); [AP via KSAT, Apr 2026](https://www.ksat.com/gallery/news/2026/04/09/japanese-town-sours-on-the-crowds-coming-to-see-cherry-blossoms-and-mount-fuji)
- Sakura season 2026 measures: security/traffic guards 1-17 April, temporary car parks, portable toilets, public transport recommended; timed rotation on the pagoda observation deck with reported waits of 1-3 hours at peak — [Fujiyoshida official sakura info](https://fujiyoshida.net/en/highlight/sakuraseason_info); [Japan Royal Service blog](https://japanroyalservice.com/blog/kawaguchiko-chureito-april-crowds)
- From April 2025 the city doubled parking fees at the Honcho 2-chome intersection (Honcho street) and at Arakurayama Sengen Park; it spends about 100 million yen a year on traffic controllers, toilets etc. Signs on Honcho street warn against stepping into the road — [Essential Japan](https://essential-japan.com/news/parking-charges-mount-fuji/); [Chris and Wren's World](https://chrisandwrensworld.com/honcho-street-fujiyoshida/)

Fees:
- The 4,000-yen fee and 4,000/day cap apply to climbing the Yoshida trail (Yamanashi), not to viewpoints or parks — [Explore-Share](https://www.explore-share.com/blog/mount-fuji-climbing-permit-rules/)

Drones:
- Drones of 100 g or more must be registered (DIPS system), carry Remote ID and display the registration mark; this applies to visitors too — [canibringto 2026](https://www.canibringto.com/blog/japan-drone-rules-registration-travel-guide-2026/); [ryukoch 2026](https://ryukoch.com/en/blogs/private-drone-usage-in-japan)
- From 14 July 2026 the no-fly buffer around designated important facilities was widened from about 300 m to about 1,000 m, for drones of any weight (single blog source) — [Japan Manners](https://japanmanners.com/rules/drone-rules-japan/)
- Above the 8.5th station (some sources say 8th) drones and paragliders are banned by the landowner, Fujisan Hongu Sengen Taisha — [Official Fuji climbing site](https://www.fujisan-climb.jp/en/things-to-know-before-climbing/)
- No blanket Fuji Five Lakes drone ordinance was found, but: lakeshores are river-law land in a national park (river occupancy permit or "riverbed/water use notification" via Yamanashi Fuji-Tobu construction office Yoshida branch; Natural Parks Act permit possible in special zones) — [Yamanashi Film Commission](https://www.yamanashi-kankou.jp/fc/fujigoko.html)
- Fujikawaguchiko town bans unmanned aircraft of any size in its city parks; Fujiyoshida does not allow drones in parks; Kirara and Hirano beach at Yamanakako need landowner permission; the Environment Ministry asks pilots in national parks to consult land managers and avoid crowded spots even where no permit is legally needed (secondary sources) — [drone guide](https://dronecompass1.com/109/); [mountain-flights.jp](https://mountain-flights.jp/fujisan/)

### Inferences
- The pattern across all hot spots is the same: the trigger is people standing in the roadway or on private land, not photography itself. Tripods on narrow pavements (Honcho street, Lawson, bridge stairs) are the behaviour most likely to draw intervention; lesser-known viewpoints avoid all of this.
- For drones the safe working assumption for a foreign visitor is: register in DIPS before travel if 100 g or more, do not fly at any named park, lakeshore event site, shrine land or above the 8th station without written permission, and expect municipal "no drone" signs at popular lake parks.

### Gaps
- Could not confirm whether the 1.4 m Lawson tarp is still in place in November 2026 (2026 travel blogs describe the view as unobstructed but are weak sources).
- Could not confirm from an official 2025/26 source that the 1.8 m Yume no Ohashi fence was built as planned, nor any fine (a "30,000 yen fine" appears only in an unsourced user review).
- No specific 2026 measure (fence, guards, closure) for Honcho street itself was found beyond parking fees and signs.
- No source found for: explicit tripod bans at named Fuji viewpoints; Fumotoppara's current reservation/day-visit/photography rules and prices (one magazine says open year-round, reservation required, solo model price 3,000 yen — [BE-PAL](https://www.bepal.net/archives/414554) — but this is not a 2026 rule check); etiquette rules for tea fields such as Obuchi Sasaba (general knowledge, unsourced: fields are private farmland, stay on paths, a donation box/paid parking during the tea season).
